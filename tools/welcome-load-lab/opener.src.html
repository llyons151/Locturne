Opener: black spreads out of the logo

The app opens on your wordmark in black, on a frosted blue sky. After a beat, black spreads out from the logo in a fast circle and covers the sky. The logo doesn’t move or zoom: the black grows out of it and swallows it. That black is the black behind the welcome, and the welcome load-in plays.

  1. 0.0 s Wordmark on the sky (the launch screen should be this same picture, so nothing jumps).
  2. 0.9 s Black spreads out from the logo, starting slow and ending fast enough to clear the corners in 0.75 s.
  3. 1.65 s All black. The phone rises in, then the words and button.
  4. 2.9 s Done.

From the centre starts the circle in the middle of the logo. From the crescent starts it inside the moon in the “c”, so the moon is where the night comes from.

Based on Duolingo’s launch on Mobbin. The wordmark is traced from your image. Only opacity and transform move, so it runs the same way on iPhone. Click the phone to replay.
