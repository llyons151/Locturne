Moon lab

Every moon on a mock of Home (from moons.json: edit them in the moon studio, npm run moon-studio). Each moon recolours the sky, the moon, its halo and the sleep orb together. To build your ladder, click a number under a moon, or hover over it and press 1–9, 0 for 10, S for the secret, X for no and ⌫ to clear. You can drag moons between slots, or from a card into a slot. Your picks save as you go.
