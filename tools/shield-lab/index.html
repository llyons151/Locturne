Shield lab

Eight takes on the screen iOS shows when you open a sleeping app. Each one shows the night shield and the morning one. iOS fixes the layout, so all we can choose is the background colour (or a blur), one icon image, the words and the button colours.

The icon size is roughly right; iOS scales the image to fit its slot, so a very wide Loc comes out smaller than drawn here. Moon picker changes takes 3, 4, 5 and 9 (their moon). Static mockups, not the app.
