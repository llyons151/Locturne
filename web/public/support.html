How it works

What does Locturne do?

At bedtime, the apps you picked go to sleep. In the morning they stay asleep until you prove you’re out of bed: by going downstairs, walking 200 steps, or scanning a code you keep in another room. Then they’re awake until the next bedtime. Apps on your always-blocked list stay blocked all the time.

Can I still get calls and texts?

Yes. iOS doesn’t let any app block phone calls, and Locturne only blocks the apps you choose. Leave anything you need at night, like messages, an authenticator or a baby monitor, off your lists.

Why didn’t my settings change right away?

Every change to your schedule, your lists or your wake-up method takes effect from the next bedtime. That’s on purpose: it means nothing can be loosened from bed at 1am. Block now is the exception: it only adds blocking, so it starts straight away.

“Go downstairs” didn’t count my stairs.

It uses your iPhone’s barometer to notice a change in height of about one floor, held for a few seconds. Start it from the app before you go, and stay on the new floor for a moment. If your phone has no barometer, or it reads flat, the app will say so. You can always tap walk 200 steps instead.

Do steps I walked before opening the app count?

Yes. Steps count from your morning start time, whether or not the app was open. Reopening the app never resets your progress.

The ways out

I’m sick, travelling or can’t get up.

Use a pass. You get 3 a month, and each one wakes your apps for the rest of the day with no walking. They refill on the 1st. Open You, then Passes.

It’s an emergency.

The emergency unlock is always available from You, after a short wait. It wakes what’s asleep tonight. Your always-blocked list stays as it is.

I can’t walk or use stairs.

Set up Scan your code instead: print or pick a code, keep it somewhere you’d go anyway, and scan it to wake your apps. Open You, then Can’t walk or use stairs. If that doesn’t work for you either, email us and we’ll help.

When something’s wrong

My apps weren’t blocked.

First check that Screen Time access is still on: the You tab says so at the top. If it was turned off in Settings, Locturne can’t block anything until it’s back on. If access was on and blocking still didn’t happen, please send us a diagnostics report (see “Get in touch”). Missed blocks are the bug we care about most.

How do I stop using Locturne?

Switch nights off in Routine, which takes effect from the next bedtime. Deleting the app also removes its blocks. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel your subscription, so cancel that too (below).

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Locturne. You can also tap Manage subscription on the app’s You tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. If you’re in the free trial, cancelling before it ends means you’re not charged.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can’t issue them ourselves, but we’re happy to help if something went wrong.

I got a new phone and Locturne wants me to pay again.

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Restore purchases on the paywall or the You tab.

Privacy

Locturne never sees which apps you block: iOS gives it sealed tokens it can’t read. Your steps, stairs and scans stay on your phone. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
