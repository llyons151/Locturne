## 1. The answer in six lines

1. **There is no secret channel.** $10K MRR is about **37,500 installs in year one**
   (~5,200 a month by month 12) at 7% paid conversion. That's about 20M
   well-targeted views. Every passed-over channel (newsletters, podcasts, schools,
   Meta ads, web funnels) was checked, and at $59.99 none of them beats the costs.
2. **Your real edge is a combination nobody else has:** a founder who can get views,
   a mechanic you can film in one shot (stairs, barometer), a character (Loc), and
   a hard paywall. Each piece alone is copyable. The combination isn't, at least not
   quickly.
3. **The biggest lesson from your past apps:** the views were real, but there was
   **no buy step and no buyer intent**. At Locturne's funnel, 1M views ≈ $450–600 of
   MRR. Content has to make a viewer think "that's me" and show the app solving it.
4. **Conversion is the biggest in-app lever.** Going from 4% to 10% of installs
   paying cuts the views you need by 60%. Renewal doesn't change month-12 MRR at all.
   It only matters from year two.
5. **The niche alone gets you about $1–6K MRR.** The rest comes from speaking to two
   bigger proven groups in the same videos: people who scroll in bed (Opal and
   one sec buyers) and people who can't get out of bed (Erly and Wayk buyers).
6. **One action has a deadline this month:** Apple's featuring nomination for a
   January launch.

## 2. Ranked edges

### Edge 1: Apple featuring for January (deadline: this month)
- App Store Connect's Featuring Nominations form has an **App Launch** type that
  covers pre-orders. Apple recommends submitting **up to 3 months ahead** for wider
  consideration, at least 2–3 weeks minimum. For early January, that's October. (V)
- You can attach an In-App Event and a **public TestFlight link**, so editors can try
  the app before launch. (V)
- "Your phone checks you actually walked downstairs" fits what Apple says it looks
  for (innovation, craft). Focus Friend won 2025's Cultural Impact award for a
  joyful anti-distraction app. (V)
- **Value:** 0 if ignored; maybe 300–2,000 paid subs if a big feature lands. With a
  rough 1-in-5 chance, that's ~60–400 expected subs for 1–2 days of work. (U)
- **Catch:** pre-orders and a public TestFlight both need an approved build, which
  needs the Family Controls *distribution* entitlement. If that isn't approved yet,
  submit the nomination anyway with a strong description and screenshots, then
  resubmit with TestFlight when it lands. Check the entitlement status first.

### Edge 2: Content built for buyers, not viewers
New case studies (Flame, Pushscroll, GlowUp, Stronger, Coconote, CutCoach) put
installs per 1K views anywhere from **0.002 to ~13**. That's a range of more than
1,000×. What separates the top from the bottom:

1. **The viewer has the problem.** "I lose an hour in bed on TikTok" beats a feature
   description.
2. **The app is the payoff on screen.** TikTok at 11pm → Loc's shield → walk
   downstairs → apps wake. If the app isn't the climax, it's a meme (Coconote's
   41M-view video sold badly because the app looked like a toy).
3. **A visible next step.** Say the name, pin a comment, use "comment *sleep* and I'll
   DM it" (Pushscroll got ~33% click-through on DMs). One 20M-view video with no
   call to action got ~1,000 installs.
4. **One format, reposted.** Single viral hits decay. Stronger made ~$600K ARR from one
   6-second format reposted ~300 times. Flame logged every hook in a spreadsheet and
   changed one variable at a time.
5. **The leading indicator is "what app?" comments per 1K views.** They show up within
   hours. Sign-ups take days.

The week-by-week plan (November hook lab → December bank over finals → January
launch → February paid creators from revenue → March decision table, each with
kill/scale numbers) is in [06 §6](edge-10k/06_indie_playbooks_content.md).

**2026 platform notes:** TikTok's US joint venture took over on January 22, 2026 and
is retraining on US data. Views per post are down ~31% in one sample while posting
volume rose. Instagram (April 2026) and Shorts (October 1, 2026) now cut reach for
reposted content, so upload natively everywhere.

### Edge 3: Wider positioning in the same videos
- Every app that locks until a morning proof has 0–5 ratings. The best one with
  verified revenue (BlockIt) makes $469 MRR. About **10 new direct rivals** launched in
  the last 60 days. The narrow niche is crowded and unproven.
- The money is next door: Opal (~$10M ARR), Wayk (~$75K/mo, launched February 2026),
  Erly ($50K+/mo), Unrot and Brainrot. (S)
- So the hooks should name the bigger pains (scrolling in bed, snoozing, being late)
  and show stairs as the *answer*, not the topic.
- **Groups nobody serves well, with real review quotes in [05 §6](edge-10k/05_competitors_market_size.md):**
  - **People who share a bed.** Mission alarms wake the partner. Locturne's unlock is
    silent. "Your partner sleeps; you just can't open TikTok until you're
    downstairs" is a claim no alarm app can make. Cheapest new hook found.
  - **Unrot refugees.** Its late-September redesign removed real-world tasks and paying
    users are furious. That's a short-term content window: "the app that still makes
    you get up".
  - **College students.** Opal reportedly says two-thirds of its daily users are
    students. You are one, at UTSA.
  - **ADHD and heavy sleepers.** Unowned, but avoid medical claims.
  - **People who delete the app to escape.** Wayk reviewers ask for it to be
    undeletable. Screen Time can block deleting apps. Decide whether Locturne
    does, and say so.

### Edge 4: Apple Ads on morning phrases (the one paid channel that works)
- At median costs, Apple Ads in Health & Fitness is ~$3.77 per install, so ~$50 per
  payer at 7.5%. That's break-even on year one and profitable over two years. Meta
  (~$104 per payer) loses money. TikTok's $50/day minimum rules out small tests. (S/U)
- Big competitors fight over "screen time" and "app blocker". Bid exact-match on
  long-tail morning phrases ("stop scrolling in bed", "alarm get out of bed") with a
  matching Custom Product Page.
- Start with Apple's free $100 credit at $5–10/day. Kill a keyword after 30 taps with
  no trial; kill the campaign above $20 per trial; scale at ≤$12 per trial. Never
  spend more than 30% of last month's net revenue.
- This is a multiplier on organic, not a replacement. Reaching $10K from ads alone
  would need ~$7–10K a month.

### Edge 5: Conversion work beats almost everything else
From the model ([03 §4](edge-10k/03_retention_ltv_pricing.md)):

| Change | Installs/month needed at month 12 |
|---|---|
| Base: $59.99, 7% paid, 75% annual | 5,210 |
| Paid conversion 4% | 9,120 |
| Paid conversion 10% | 3,650 |
| Strong product + 14-day trial (9%, 40% renewal) | 4,050 |
| $39.99 at the same conversion | 7,060 |

- Each extra point of paywall conversion around 7% saves ~650 installs a month.
- **Price:** $59.99 is the low-regret choice. Cheaper only wins if it converts
  proportionally better. But reviewers in this category keep saying "a subscription
  for an alarm?", so the paywall must sell a *sleep and phone* product, not an alarm.
- **Test 14-day vs 7-day trials** on the main paywall (already in 10K_MRR_PLAN §5).
- **Year-one cash:** about $3 net per install, because annual plans pay upfront. The
  base case collects ~$115K in year one even though MRR only reaches $10K at the end.
  For your income needs, that is the number to watch.

### Edge 6: Retention as a year-two weapon
- The cliff is real: hit exactly $10K at month 12, stop all installs, and at 25%
  renewal MRR is **$2.4K by month 24**.
- Blockers churn *because they work*: people feel they've graduated. Frame the morning
  lock as **a permanent tool, like an alarm clock**, not a 30-day course.
- Cheap levers, ranked:
  1. **Retention Messaging API:** Loc speaks on Apple's own cancel screen, including
     trial cancels. 1–2 dev days. RevenueCat reports big save rates in Apple's beta
     (vendor figure).
  2. **"Loc's year" recap** 7–14 days before each annual renewal, plus Billing Grace
     Period.
  3. **Wake buddy:** one person who only hears good news ("Sam's up."). A missed
     morning stays private. Duolingo's Friend Streak raised daily completion 22% (V).
     This fits the buddy/couples mode already planned for v1.1. It's one person, not
     a squad, so it doesn't break the "no squads or leaderboards" rule.
  4. **Partner seats via Apple's Group Purchases** ("this winter", date unconfirmed),
     which pairs with the shared-bed angle.

## 3. Checked and rejected (so you don't have to)

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Web paywall / Stripe link-out at launch | Saves ~$6 per annual sale at your 15% rate. Tests show conversion drops 5–26% and refunds rise. Apple may also add a 5–15% link-out fee after the Supreme Court case. Add RevenueCat Web Billing at ~$2K MRR, for email win-backs and creator links only. |
| Meta / TikTok ads | Meta ~$104 per payer vs ~$50 earned. TikTok's minimum is $50/day. |
| Newsletter, podcast, YouTube sponsorships | $80–250 per paying sub at today's CPMs. Use pay-on-sales affiliates (one sec pays 40% of the first year) instead. |
| Selling to universities, employers, clinicians | Apple rule 3.1.3(c) blocks selling outside in-app purchase unless you *only* sell to organisations. Volume purchasing doesn't support subscriptions. Free offer codes (up to 25,000 redemptions per batch) still work for a UTSA dorm challenge, mostly as filming material. |
| Android in year one | 0.9% install→paid vs 2.6% on iOS, Play restricts Accessibility-based blocking, and many cheaper phones have no barometer. ~20–30 dev days. A website waitlist is enough. |
| Weekly plans, pay-what-you-want, student discount at launch, money stakes | Weekly pricing is already ruled out in GAME_PLAN. The rest lowers revenue or breaks the no-punishment rule. |
| Faceless account farms | The verified failure pattern in this niche (Reveille: 3M views, $121 MRR). |

**Platform risk check:** iOS 27 doesn't copy Locturne. Apple's 2026 Screen Time work
is for parents of children, and its sleep work is advice (a Readiness score, wind-down
suggestions), not locks. DeviceActivity is still unreliable, so "never fails silently"
remains a real selling point.

**Biggest competitive threat:** Erly. It already has the "get out of bed" buyers and
camera proof, and a screenshot reportedly shows a "Bedtime App Lock" (S). Check its
App Store page monthly, along with Anchor Morning ($39.99/yr, no trial, launched
September 13).

## 5. Decisions for you

1. **Uninstall protection:** should Locturne stop people deleting it during the night
   lock? Users ask for it. It needs a clear escape (emergency unlock) so it's never a
   trap.
2. **Lead hook additions:** add "your partner keeps sleeping" (the silent unlock) and
   "the app that still makes you get up" (Unrot refugees) to the November lab?
3. **The wake buddy** as the first piece of v1.1 buddy mode: good news only, misses
   stay private.
4. **Facing the camera daily.** Every new case study that reached $10K leaned on a
   face or paid creators. Is that OK for you, and can a roommate film two mornings a
   week?

## 6. Caveats

- Most revenue figures are founder- or vendor-reported (S). Survivorship bias is
  heavy: the case studies are winners.
- Two research passes ran out of search budget, so Reddit threads, shift workers and
  TikTok's 2026 link rules are thin. Those gaps are flagged in the files.
- The MRR model's conversion-vs-price curve is a guess. Replace it with real numbers
  after launch.
- [03](edge-10k/03_retention_ltv_pricing.md) §5 mentions a $99.99 lifetime plan.
  Lifetime was dropped from GAME_PLAN on 2026-09-26, so ignore that row unless it's
  revived. (If it is, price it at $129.99–149.99: an annual buyer is worth ~$102.)
