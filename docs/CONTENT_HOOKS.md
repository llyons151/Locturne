# Content Hooks

Hooks for personal IG videos about building apps (general app creation, not
Locturne promotion). Goal of the account: practice articulation and camera
skills. Track skill (fewer takes, less "um", watchable on rewatch), not views.

## Picked hooks

1. **"Everyone says ship fast. I think that's wrong."**
   Contrarian. Needs a real reason right after the hook (e.g. shipping
   something that looks generic gets dismissed as slop; taste takes time).
2. **"You don't need to know how to code to start building apps. You need to know this."**
   Contrarian + curiosity gap. Payoff must be one concrete thing, said fast.
3. **"How to make your app feel smooth in 10 seconds."**
   Quick tip. Show a before/after on screen; deliver in under 10s as promised.
4. **"What building an app alone actually looks like."**
   Behind the scenes. Real, messy screen recording of a work session.
5. **"Stop watching coding tutorials. Do this instead."**
   Contrarian. Payoff: one concrete alternative (e.g. build a small real thing
   and look things up as you get stuck).
6. **"Nobody cares about your tech stack."**
   Contrarian. Follow with what users actually notice (how it looks and feels).
7. **"Why your app looks cheap, in one sentence."**
   Quick tip. Must actually be one sentence, then show an example.
8. **"Things that broke this week building my app."**
   What went wrong. Recurring weekly format; quick cuts of each bug.
9. **"A day of building an app as a full-time student."**
   Behind the scenes. Mix of class, study, and building clips.

## Hook rules

- Say the hook in the first second. No intro, no "hey guys".
- Put the hook on screen as text too (sound-off viewers).
- Make the first frame interesting to look at (face mid-sentence, or the screen).
- Pay off the hook immediately after it.

## Filming approach

- Plan only the first line and the point; say the rest in your own words.
- Talk to one specific person, not "the audience".
- If talking to camera is hard, screen-record and narrate over it.
- After each video, pick one thing to fix next time (filler words, eye
  contact, energy at the start, pace).
