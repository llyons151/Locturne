The pitch angle that fits nearly every target: *"Most blockers let you tap 'ignore limit'. Locturne keeps your apps asleep until your body is out of bed: 200 steps, a walk downstairs, push-ups or a scan. A solo student built it."* Movement-gated unlocking is a category TechCrunch already covers (see a1), so lead with that.

Editorial outlets rarely change a published roundup because someone asks. Ask instead to be considered when they **refresh** the piece; most "best X" posts get updated once or twice a year.

## Suggested order (fastest editorial wins)

1. **9to5Mac Indie App Spotlight (c1) and Indie Dev Monday (c2).** Both take email, both are free, and both featured apps in the past week.
2. **University newsroom and student paper (f1, f3).** A student-founder story has the highest odds of any target here.
3. **TechCrunch: Lauren Forristal (a1).** Exact category match. Pitch as a launch, or for the next refresh.
4. **BGR: Khamosh Pathak (a6).** The Brick-alternatives angle.
5. **MakeUseOf alarm lists (a11, a12, a17).** Direct "forces you out of bed" fit.
6. **Qwoted and Source of Sources (e4, e5) plus a daily #journorequest check.** These are how you get into Sleep Foundation, Tom's Guide sleep and Woman & Home.
7. **ADDitude submission (b2) and Zapier pitch (b1).** These two take the longest, but carry the most authority.
8. **Swift Student Challenge (f8).** Put it in the calendar for around February 2027.
