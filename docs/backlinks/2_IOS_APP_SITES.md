# Locturne: iOS and app-specific backlink and discovery targets

Researched 2026-10-12. "Alive" means it was fetched today and showed 2026-dated content or a working submit form.
Authority is a rough guess (High is roughly DR 70+, Med roughly DR 40-70, Low is under that), not a measured score.
Link type: "dofollow" or "nofollow" only where known or very likely. "Editorial" means an in-article link that is usually followed, but the editor decides.
Nothing was submitted.

**Pitch angle for editorial sites:** an indie student-built alarm that keeps your apps asleep until you're physically up
(200 steps, walking downstairs measured with the barometer, Vision push-ups). Send it with the App Store link, a press kit
and a 20-second screen recording. Send pitches in the week before launch, and again with an update or a holiday deal.
