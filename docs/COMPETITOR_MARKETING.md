# Competitor marketing: what works and how Locturne copies it

Researched October 5, 2026: five parallel passes covering screen-time apps, hardware
and TikTok-native blockers, alarm and sleep apps, mascot-led apps, and every direct
competitor. It builds on the competitor list in
[VALIDATION_RESEARCH.md](VALIDATION_RESEARCH.md) §3 and the slideshow plan in
[SLIDESHOW_MARKETING.md](SLIDESHOW_MARKETING.md). GAME_PLAN stays the source of truth;
the plan in §5 fits its "build first, then real-footage videos" rule.

Grades: **V** verified (primary source, company figures or reputable press with
numbers), **S** secondhand, **U** unverified or estimate.

## 1. Short version

1. **Nobody owns this niche yet.** Every direct "lock overnight, unlock after a morning
   action" app has under 10 ratings. No app uses stairs or a barometer.
2. **Category winners grew from one of three engines:** paid niche creators at
   $2–3 per 1,000 views (Erly, QUITTR, Cal AI, Alarmy), a person-led content channel
   (Opal's Olivia Unplugged, Brainrot's founder vlogs), or one clip that shows the
   mechanic in five seconds (one sec, Cat Gatekeeper, Clearspace).
3. **A character multiplies all three.** Duolingo's posts without the owl got 20–30K
   views; with the owl, 1M+ (S). Duolingo told investors DAU growth slowed when it posted
   less owl content (V).
4. **The App Store keywords for sleep, bedtime and morning are empty** among the big
   blockers. "Walk to unlock" is saturated (25+ titles); "get out of bed" is used only by
   alarm apps.
5. **The real threats:** Erly or Wayk adding app locks (they own the wake-up audience),
   and ScreenZen Halo, a $49 bedside puck sold as "no subscription" (June 2026).

## 3. What's working, and why

### 3.1 Paid niche creators on view guarantees (strongest evidence)
- **Erly** (solo founder, closest analogue): $0 → ~$50K/month in 4 months, ~200K
  downloads, almost entirely paid "day in the life" creators at $2–3 CPM, 2–4 posts a
  month each, view-guaranteed. 1–5M-view breakouts pay for the rest. Tracked by matching
  post times to sign-up spikes (S, [superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/case-study/erly)).
- **QUITTR:** $250K MRR in ~4 months. Started with Christian creators, then fitness and
  self-help. $50 promo → ~$1K/day; month one $3K → $37K. Deals like $1,500 per 500K views,
  prorated if short; 20% upfront. "Millions of views, poor conversions; 25K views,
  excellent" (S, [StartupSpells](https://startupspells.com/p/porn-addiction-app-quittr-250k-mrr-4-months)).
- **Cal AI:** assistants DM 50+ creators a day; 4-video bundles; the app appears
  inside native formats like "What I Eat in a Day" (S).
- **Alarmy:** TikTok Creative Challenge, 150+ creator videos, 8.8M views, cost per
  install −46% (V, [TikTok case study](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/alarmy)).
- **Why it works:** creators' audiences already trust them, niche audiences convert
  better than big ones, and the app shows up inside a format people already watch.
  Paying for views, not posts, caps the risk.

### 3.2 A person-led content channel
- **Opal:** hired a user, Olivia, to run "Olivia Unplugged": whiteboard lessons on
  tiredness and phone habits, about 3 a week, bio "Powered by Opal". 700K followers in a
  few months, 8M views a month, top video 2.6M ("How to stop feeling tired all the
  time"). Licensed as an ad, one reached tens of millions. "The best ad was never
  explicitly about Opal" (S, [Link in Bio](https://www.linkinbio.news/p/creator-brand-olivia-unplugged-opal)).
- **Brainrot:** 8+ months of daily founder vlogs to ~200K followers, then Product Hunt
  #1 → 5K downloads on day one, $26K in 30 days. "Your personal brand will last longer
  than your product" (S).
- **Why it works:** people follow people. Teaching about the problem reaches everyone
  who has it, not just people searching for an app.

### 3.3 One clip that shows the whole mechanic
- **one sec:** the founder had ~700 followers when a plain screen recording of the
  breathing pause went viral; it carried growth for months (V, founder interview).
- **Cat Gatekeeper:** a cat lying across your screen when time's up spread on X and
  TikTok into PopSci, PCWorld and Fast Company (V coverage).
- **Brick:** the tap became a verb, "bricking" (S). **Clearspace:** "everyone would be
  ripped in 3 months" (push-ups for minutes).
- **touch grass:** a meme name, a 1.2M-view tweet, a 4M-view short, Hacker News #1,
  TechCrunch (S).
- **Why it works:** the viewer understands the product, the joke and the problem in one
  frame. Surprise + a shared pain + something to argue about in the comments.

### 3.4 The character as the creator
- **Duolingo:** owl vs no owl, 1M+ vs 20–30K views (S). Comment marketing added 1.5M
  followers in two months; one troll comment reached 3.9M views as a screen recording (S).
  The "Duo is dead" stunt gave the best iOS download day of the year (V).
- **Finch, Pixel Pals:** the character lives where people see it (screenshots, widgets,
  Dynamic Island); Pixel Pals 3.3M installs (V).
- **What fails:** one-off stunts don't stick; audiences spot "pre-approved chaos"; the
  character takes the hit for company decisions (Duolingo's AI backlash).

### 3.5 The morning struggle on camera
- **Alarmy:** partners and roommates filming someone half-asleep doing the mission. One
  video: 7M+ views and 33K installs in 24 hours (S); a repeatable format hit 24M (S).
- **Why it works:** everyone shares the pain, the half-awake person is funny, and the
  filmer supplies the reaction.

### 3.6 Store, product and press
- **Apple editorial:** Jomo, two people, no ads: six App Store features (V).
- **Store listing:** Alarmy's tested, localised listings: +91% visitors, +26% conversion
  (V, [Google case study](https://developer.android.com/stories/apps/alarmy)). AppBlock in 15
  languages.
- **Seasonality:** Rise Science spends most in January and August (S). TechCrunch runs
  New Year app roundups.
- **In-product sharing:** Opal's referral tiers (invite 5 → a free year; a progress bar
  raised senders 80%, S), a "lifetime screen time" shock screen before the paywall (trial
  starts 7% → 17%, S), and "where did you hear about us?" in onboarding.
- **Credibility:** one sec's peer-reviewed study (−57% app use) brought press,
  universities and therapist referrals (V).
- **SEO:** Jomo and WalkBlock write "how to block TikTok on iPhone" style guides.

### What to skip
- Paid Meta ads (Opal, Brick, Rise): they work, but need budget and a proven hook.
- Going free (ScreenZen): incompatible with a hard paywall.
- Sock-puppet tactics: QUITTR's fake Reddit ask-and-answer, Cal AI's seeded comments,
  faceless "I found this app" accounts. FTC and ban risk ([SLIDESHOW_MARKETING.md](SLIDESHOW_MARKETING.md) §6).
- Costumes, celebrity launches (Focus Friend's #1 faded in two weeks), national ads.

## 4. Locturne's angles

- **The clip:** tap TikTok at 11pm → Loc's shield, "asleep" → 7am, walk downstairs →
  the apps wake up. That's one sec's breathing pause and Brick's tap in one shot.
- **The verb:** "my apps are asleep" / "put your apps to bed". Both phrases are unclaimed.
- **The character:** Loc in every video and every shield, dry and teasing, never guilt.
- **The gap:** both ends of the night in one app, and stairs nobody else uses.
- **Against Halo's "no subscription":** a coffee a month for your mornings, and no puck
  to lose or leave in another room.

## 5. The plan

Budget assumption: $0–100 a month until launch revenue, then reinvest. Every channel is
measured by **waitlist sign-ups or trial starts per 1K views**, never views alone.

### Now → mid-November (while the device test runs)
1. **Store listing draft.** Title and subtitle around "get out of bed", "apps sleep",
   "bedtime", "morning". Keyword field: "morning app blocker", "stop scrolling in bed",
   "phone in bed", "stairs". Avoid leading with "walk to unlock".
2. **Loc kit.** One master reference sheet (front, 3/4, about 6 expressions) and ~20
   reusable PNG poses and stickers, so Loc looks the same in every post at no cost per
   post ([MASCOT_DIRECTION.md](MASCOT_DIRECTION.md)).
3. **Accounts.** Reserve the handles now; start the 5–7 day warm-up in late October.
4. **"Where did you hear about us?"** in onboarding, so January's data says which
   channel works.
5. **Shareable morning card** and a simple referral (e.g. invite 3 → a free month) on
   the v1 list, if time allows; they cost nothing per user later.

### November 1 → launch (organic, $0)
6. **The mechanic clip**, from the first day there's real footage: one plain screen
   recording of the apps falling asleep and waking after the stairs. Post it on TikTok,
   Reels, Shorts and X.
7. **Founder account, person-led** (Opal + Brainrot): "student building an app that
   puts my apps to sleep", plus Olivia-style teaching posts about the problem (revenge
   bedtime procrastination, why you're tired, phones in bed), drawing on
   [NIGHT_PHONE_SCIENCE.md](NIGHT_PHONE_SCIENCE.md). Mention Locturne lightly.
8. **Loc account, character-led** (Duolingo): Loc reacting to trends, Loc on the
   shield, and **10 minutes a day of comments as Loc** on viral sleep, doomscrolling and
   "bed rotting" videos. Dry, never selling in the comment.
9. **Morning-struggle format** (Alarmy): a friend or roommate filming the half-awake
   walk downstairs. Make 3–5 fixed templates and repeat the ones that work.
10. **Slideshow hook lab** alongside, per [SLIDESHOW_MARKETING.md](SLIDESHOW_MARKETING.md).
11. **CTA:** "launches in January" + the locturne.com waitlist, tagged per account and
    format. App Store pre-order if review passes early.

### December → launch week (New Year)
12. **Apple featuring nomination** for January (resolutions, sleep), plus an in-app
    event. Jomo got six features this way.
13. **Press pitch:** "the app that won't let you open TikTok until you walk downstairs".
    TechCrunch has run two roundups of this category (Jan 2025, Aug 2026). Pitch the New
    Year app lists too.
14. **Product Hunt and r/nosurf, honestly**, in launch week. Brainrot's PH #1 brought
    5K downloads on day one; BedLock's got 1 upvote. The difference was an existing
    audience, so launch PH only after the accounts have some.
15. **"New year, phone stays asleep till 7am"** content, timed to the first week of
    January.

### After launch (from first revenue)
16. **Paid micro-creators** (Erly, QUITTR): start with 2–3 test deals of $50–150 each in
    morning-routine, "that girl", studytok, self-improvement and Christian-discipline
    niches. Pay $2–3 CPM with view guarantees, prorated; 4-video bundles. Give them the
    template that won organically. Keep a creator only if their installs or trials per 1K
    views beat the organic average.
17. **Licensed creator ads:** when an organic post wins, put a small budget behind it
    (Opal's best ad started as an organic post).
18. **Localise the listing** into the top 5 markets (Alarmy, AppBlock).
19. **SEO guides** on locturne.com: "how to stop scrolling in bed", "apps that lock your
    phone until you get out of bed".
20. **August:** back-to-school push (Rise's second spend peak).

### Decision rules
- After ~30 posts per account, put the time into whichever account and format has the
  best sign-ups per 1K views; drop the rest.
- A hook that wins in slideshows gets filmed for real.
- Scale creator spend only from revenue, never ahead of it.
