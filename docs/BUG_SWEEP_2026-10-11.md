# Bug sweep 2026-10-11: everything since the last iOS build

Scope: every change since EAS build `ce902f65` (commit `c98c644`, 2026-10-09, development profile) through
`8b55429`, plus the uncommitted push-ups work. That is ~191 files and ~13K lines: camera push-ups (new `pose-camera`
module, `proximity` removed), Get to a place (new `place-search` module, `expo-location`), the Apps tab and limits
rework with websites, Loc on Home, the You tab, Routine, onboarding and the paywall.

Method: automated checks, then six parallel read-only reviews (push-ups, place, Apps, Home/Loc/You, Routine/onboarding,
build readiness plus a web smoke crawl), then each reviewer fixed its own findings in its own files.

## Fixed

**Push-ups**
- The 10-minute timeout ran from when the camera opened and wiped the reps done. Now it counts 10 minutes with no plank in view, and "Start again" keeps the reps. The timed-out line says how many are left.
- The Swift frame throttle (1/15 s) landed on every third frame (~10 fps). It is now 0.06 s, so it really runs at ~15 fps.
- A goal change mid-set restarted the session from 0. Now `retarget` keeps the reps, applied on the clock tick.
- A camera restricted by Screen Time or a profile has its own state: no Open Settings, just walk instead. Returning from Settings with access granted shows Start.
- The Swift camera restarted at most once ever and could restart after the set ended. Now it restarts only if frames flowed since the last restart, and only while it is wanted.
- The `dom` prop is memoised; it was a new object every frame.

**Get to a place**
- "Use where I am now" saved fixes of any accuracy (Approximate Location could be off by km). It now needs a sharp fix.
- Approximate Location gave "Can't tell yet" forever. It now has its own reason: "Turn on Precise Location" plus Open Settings.
- Restricted location has its own copy, read from iOS via the new `locationAccess`.
- Search is biased to a quick low-accuracy fix when permission was already granted, rows show distances, and offline is no longer "Nothing found".
- Cancelled MapKit searches always resolve, old snapshot files are deleted, and the snapshotter is retained until it finishes.
- A sheet left open across bedtime now shows "Not from bed" on save.
- `app.json`: `locationAlwaysPermission: false` removes Expo's generic Always-location purpose string.

**Apps tab and limits**
- A removed website is now redated with the apps removed alongside it (`delaySiteChanges` in `redateLooserEdits`). Native test added.
- A rename survives a refused arm and a settle (`keepName`).
- The limit sheet's Apps row no longer drops unsaved edits: a name saves silently, and a time change asks first.
- "Remove limit" on the list page confirms first, like the sheet does.
- The limit tile uses `startsLabel` instead of a hard-coded "tonight".
- The websites card says when Screen Time access is off and refuses to add.
- Web preview limits are keyed by id, not index.

**Home, Loc, You**
- Loc's WebGL frame loop stopped only on unmount. It now stops when Loc is hidden and idle, or the page is hidden.
- The wake-done and hello lines played under the Wake modal and were lost. They now wait until Home is focused.
- The bubble is capped to the room above Home's button: fewer lines and shrink-to-fit at large text.
- "Said once" persists across relaunches (`locturne.loc.said`).
- Burrow keyframes stay in order. Reduce Motion fades Loc in place instead of launching him. The arrival timer is cleared. Subscriptions are stable. The dev push-ups card is `__DEV__`-required.

**Routine and onboarding**
- The push-ups Motion reason was stale: it mentioned steps before push-ups. It now names the walk fallback.
- Onboarding copy follows the custom push-up count and step goal (`morningGoals`, `moreMethods`, `walkCopy`, `pushupsDemo`).
- The "tonight" day label after midnight names the night in progress.
- The paywall falls back to `steps`, not `downstairs`. The `hasRoutine()` guard is restored.

## Known, not fixed

- **Place:** big venues are judged against one 120 m circle around the MapKit centre point. `/wake` and the shield can name different methods during a waiting edit's first night (this predates the sweep). Any proof unlocks.
- **Push-ups:** the audio session toggles around every spoken rep. Android would get the web camera view (iOS only today).
- **Loc:** the full-width Loc band takes touches on Home.
- **Routine:** Shadow Loc replays on every Routine visit (marked TESTING). This needs a release decision.
