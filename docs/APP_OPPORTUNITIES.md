Written October 3, 2026. This document ranks app opportunities for a possible pivot after
launch, or for a second app. It combines five research passes, which are stored in

or third-party estimates, so treat them as peak months, often gross.

## 1. The answer in five lines

1. **Don't pivot before launch.** Locturne's category (an alarm with a physical task)
   was one of 2026's breakout categories. Erly reached about $50K/mo by month 4 and
   Wayk is estimated at about $75K/mo. It is now contested, though, and clones arrive
   4–8 weeks after anyone shows traction.
2. **The best pivot is a second app on the Locturne engine:** a sports-betting
   quit app for young men. It has strong evidence of pain, a proven formula (Quittr),
   fragmented competition, and it reuses the Screen Time blocking, onboarding and
   paywall code.
3. **The best hedge where retention doesn't matter** is test prep for one exam with a
   deadline. People pay, pass and leave, and it grows through App Store search rather
   than video, so it complements the video-driven apps.
4. **The best use of your proven skill (fandom daily games)** is a portfolio of
   ad-supported daily web games on long-lived fandoms. The cash comes slowly, and it
   works as a side income and a funnel to your apps, not as a replacement.
5. **None of these reliably closes a $1.5–2K/mo gap within weeks.** Only 17% of new
   subscription apps ever reach $1K MRR, and the median for those that do is 58 days.
   A part-time job or Expo contract work is still the income floor; apps are the upside.

## 2. What the market looks like right now

- **Distribution is the product.** Almost every breakout found one repeatable
  short-form format and ran it at volume:
  - **Owned accounts:** Bible BFF ran 49 of its own TikTok accounts.
  - **Cheap creators:** Jungle had 30 creators making about 400 videos a week; Erly
    and Quittr paid creators $2–4 per 1K views.
  - **Founder volume:** GoTall's solo Expo developer posted about 10 TikToks a day.

  Faceless slideshow templates are saturated: Metricool's 2026 study found TikTok
  reach down about 30% year on year. Founder-on-camera, real-footage video is still
  an edge.
- **Hard paywall after a quiz is the norm.** It converts 10.7% of downloads to paid
  by day 35, against 2.1% for freemium, with about the same one-year retention
  (RevenueCat 2026).
- **Mechanics that are winning:**
  - Self-control you prove physically on camera (Erly, Pushscroll, Wayk).
  - Self-improvement for young men (quitting, height, social skills).
  - Faith (Hallow about $40M in 2025, Bible BFF).
  - "Point the camera, get an AI verdict."
  - Student study tools.
  - Passionate hobby niches.
- **Saturated, avoid as a primary bet:**
  - AI calorie scanners (Cal AI was bought by MyFitnessPal).
  - Quit-porn apps (Quittr plus at least 5 clones).
  - Looksmax and rizz AI, AI Bible chat, AI headshots, photo enhancers and homework
    solvers.
  - AI companions.
  - Generic screen-time blockers: 204 exist, 103 of them launched since January 2025.
- **Durability is weak everywhere.**
  - Subscription launches now run at about 14.7K a month (7x 2022), and apps
    launched since 2025 earn about 3% of subscription revenue.
  - AI apps churn 30% faster.
  - Many winners sold early: Cal AI, Coconote, Unchaind.
- **New App Store risk.** Since June 9, 2026, guideline 4.3(b) lets Apple remove live
  apps that are "indistinguishable" from existing ones. Reskins of one engine have to
  be genuinely different products.

## 4. The top opportunities in detail

### 1. Sports-betting quit app for young men (best pivot)

- **Pain:** Coverage from CNN and NPR (Feb 2026) on young men and sports-betting
  addiction. Legal betting keeps spreading by state.
- **Proven formula:** Quittr (quit porn) reached about $250K MRR in about 5 months at
  roughly $45/yr, with about 25% of downloads paying. It sold through memes and
  retainer creators at $3 per 1K views.
- **Competition:** about a dozen small gambling apps at $35–50/yr. None dominates, and
  none has public revenue.
- **Trust opening:** Quittr leaked relapse data for about 600K users (reported Jan
  2026). "Your data never leaves your phone" is a real selling point.
- **Your edge:**
  - Real Screen Time blocking of sportsbook apps, which most small competitors can't do.
  - A harder lock on game days.
  - A counter of money not bet, which makes a natural share card.
  - The gamer and young-male audience overlaps with your fandom videos.
- **Risks:**
  - No revenue proof for any app in this exact niche.
  - Sensitive audience: avoid relapse shaming, link to the 1-800-GAMBLER helpline.
  - It must be distinct enough from Locturne to pass 4.3(b).
- **Build:** about 4–6 weeks on the Locturne engine.

### 2. Test prep for one exam with a deadline (best retention-proof hedge)

- **Willingness to pay:** Zutobi (driving permit tests) is estimated at about
  $300–400K/mo. NCLEX prep sells for $139–249, and students complain about the price.
- **Why it suits you:**
  - People pay before the exam and leave after it, so churn is the design, not a
    failure.
  - It grows through App Store search ("[state] permit test"), which spreads your risk
    beyond video.
- **Wedge:** one state's permit test, or the CNA exam, done properly. Charge a single
  "until you pass" price, or a weekly plan.
- **Risks:**
  - The content has to be accurate; AI-generated questions need checking against the
    official handbook.
  - Expanding to other states or exams adds content work for each one.
  - Video fit is weaker.

### 3. Fandom daily-game portfolio (best use of a skill you've already proven)

- **Proof:** one solo developer lives off LoLdle, Pokedle, Dotadle and Smashdle.
  LoLdle gets about 5M pageviews a month; I estimate $10–30K/mo, which is not a
  reported figure.
- **What $1.5–2K/mo takes:** about 400–650K pageviews a month.
- **Why Rivaldle leaked:** Marvel Rivals lost about 85% of its peak players. A daily
  game can't outgrow its fandom.
- **The fix:**
  - Pick large, long-lived fandoms and run several of them off one engine.
  - Offer 4–6 modes a day, since each mode is another pageview.
  - Add streaks and a share grid.
  - Turn each day's puzzle into a short video.
- **Rules and limits:**
  - Riot allows ad revenue but forbids Patreon-style paywalls and running the site as
    a business entity.
  - Nobody has publicly sold a game built on someone else's franchise.
  - The NYT sends takedowns over the Wordle format.
- **Role:** low-maintenance side income and a way to send gamers to your apps.

### 4. "Scripture before scroll" faith lock

> **Update (Oct 3, 2026):** the follow-up research in [FAITH_LOCK_RESEARCH.md](FAITH_LOCK_RESEARCH.md)
> found about 300 faith screen-time apps (Bible Mode 12.4K ratings, BibleScroll already
> unlocks after time spent in YouVersion, and Bible Chat launched a lock on Oct 2, 2026),
> so openness drops from 3 to 1. The best remaining angle is a faith morning mode inside
> Locturne.

- **Willingness to pay:** Hallow made about $40M net in 2025 and Bible Chat about
  $15M/yr. Bible BFF reached $60K MRR within a month. US Bible sales rose 14% in 2025.
- **Wedge:** social apps stay locked until you read today's verse. It reuses about 75%
  of the Locturne engine.
- **Caveats:**
  - Similar apps probably exist; search the App Store before building.
  - It only works if you can make faith content authentically, or partner with a
    creator who can.

### 5. Trading-card collector tool

- **Market:**
  - The Pokémon Company had a record year (about $3.3B).
  - Collectr makes about $400K a month (Sensor Tower snippet) and has 2M+ users.
  - Pack-opening is native short-form video content.
- **Wedge:** a pull-rate log ("what did my packs actually return") or master-set
  tracking, not another price scanner.
- **Risks:** card price data costs money, and the scanner category is crowded.

## 5. Platform windows worth knowing

- **Apple's free cloud AI (iOS 27):**
  - Small Business Program developers with under 2M downloads can use Apple's server
    model, including image input, at no per-call cost. Access is by request, and
    Apple publishes no rate limits.
  - It makes "photo in, answer out" apps viable at zero inference cost. I'd expect the
    window to last 9–18 months.
  - **Action: request access now.** It also suits a free "Loc" morning line.
- **Siri and App Intents (shipped Sept 14, 2026):** cheap to add, and "Siri, set my
  Locturne alarm" makes a good video. Claims that it helps discovery are unproven.
- **AlarmKit:**
  - **Wake-up alarms are now crowded:** Erly, Wayk, Awake, AlarmK, Alarmy and others.
  - **Niche alarms are still open for about a year:** GLP-1 shot day, medication for
    older parents, task alarms for people with ADHD.
- **Low value for you:**
  - **US web checkout:** a rate is being set, and it is roughly fee-neutral for a 15%
    developer.
  - **EU DMA terms.**
  - **Age-verification laws:** they create compliance work, not demand. Texas is in
    force and requires parental approval for under-18 purchases.
  - **Meta glasses:** closed to 100 testers.
  - **Vision Pro.**

## 6. Avoid

- AI-as-the-product subscriptions: high churn, and the worst fit for your retention
  weakness.
- Mobile puzzle or hybrid-casual games: they need paid ads to grow.
- Parental controls: one per device, entrenched incumbents, and new age laws.
- B2B micro-SaaS, Chrome extensions, Shopify apps: 8–18 months to $1K, and most never
  get there.
- Anything with a toggle paywall or a fake countdown timer: Apple has been rejecting
  these since January 2026.

## 7. What this means for Locturne now

1. **Price check.** Erly anchors the alarm niche at $29.99/yr, while Locturne plans
   $59.99. Higher prices do earn more per payer (RevenueCat), so this isn't
   automatically wrong. Plan an A/B test, and make sure the night-plus-morning loop
   visibly justifies twice the price.
2. **Compete on retention.** Erly's founder names retention as the moat. Streaks, a
   difficulty curve and low annoyance answer both your leaky-bucket history and the
   clones.
3. **Build Locturne as an engine.** Keep the blocking, quiz onboarding, paywall and
   voice system modular, so the betting or faith app is a 4–6 week build, not a
   rewrite.
4. **Pivot triggers, 60 days after launch:**
   - Video works, but payers per 1K views stay under about 0.3: the problem is the
     product, so pivot within the engine (#1 or #4).
   - Video itself doesn't work: the problem is distribution, so prefer the exam-prep
     hedge (#2), which grows through search.
5. **Cheap side bets now:**
   - Request Apple's free cloud AI access.
   - If you have spare evenings, put one long-lived-fandom daily game on the existing
     Rivaldle engine.
