# Whole-store market scan: proven demand + open market

Written October 4, 2026. You asked for the highest-value app you could make, with demand
that's proven and a market that's still open, after the faith-lock idea turned out to
have about 300 competitors. This document supersedes the rankings in
[APP_OPPORTUNITIES.md](APP_OPPORTUNITIES.md) and
[FAITH_LOCK_RESEARCH.md](FAITH_LOCK_RESEARCH.md).

The raw work is in [market-scan/](market-scan/):

- the rubric
- 5 category hunts
- 4 red-team reports, which tried to disprove each finalist
- the chart data
- the scanner tools

## 1. The answer

**No niche passed.** That covers:

- About 100 niches scanned against live App Store data.
- 25 candidates that looked promising from the charts.
- 13 finalists red-teamed in depth.

All of them were killed. That isn't a failure of the search. It is the most useful
finding, because it changes the question you should be asking.

**Any niche you can see making money on the charts is visible to everyone, and it gets
cloned within 1–6 months.** Template studios, often several of them, ship the obvious
improvement first.

The money stays with:

1. **The first app into a new niche:** Game Maps IRL, WBuilds, Imposter Game, Track N
   Trail, Erly and Picolo.
2. **Studios that buy their installs with paid ads:** the Starbucks secret-menu app, and
   the meal planners below.

Followers with no distribution of their own stall below about 150 ratings, again and again.

**So the highest-value app for you can't be found as a gap on the charts. You have to be
first.** Your real advantage is the one thing the clone studios lack: you can make an
idea go viral yourself. The winning process is:

1. Test new concepts with videos.
2. Ship in 2–3 weeks.
3. Collect the money during the window before the clones arrive.
4. Use retention features to stay ahead after that.

## 3. What a niche opening looks like in real time

Supermarket budget meal planners show the full cycle in four months:

- **June 2026:** mise and Potto launch.
- **Within weeks:** they reach Food & Drink #3 and #7 in top-grossing.
- **By September:** TapCook (#5), NeatEat (#37), Whipp, Herbi, Chow and Vego are all on
  the chart.

Several followers *did* chart here, but only because this is a paid-ad game with a big
market. **The window between "it's on the chart" and "it's flooded" is weeks for big
markets and a few months for small ones.**

Other niches that have opened since June 2026, unscanned, for reference only:

- penny-deal and clearance finders (Drop at Shopping #22, Scavenger)
- AI coding-agent usage trackers (Limits, Nowdex)
- TCG drop alerts (DropSync)
- social drive trackers (Wheelz at Navigation #43)
- Bible video shorts (Bible Shorts at Reference #33)
- ASL learning (Ziggy at Education #33)

If a niche is already on this list, you're already late to it.

## 4. What wins for you

The same pattern runs through every killed candidate and every winner.

1. **Originality plus distribution beats picking gaps.** The winners in this data were
   the first app in their niche (Game Maps IRL, WBuilds, Erly, Imposter Game, Track N
   Trail), or caught a trend early and rode one viral format (Imposter Game on the 2025
   TikTok imposter trend). You have the rare half of that, the ability to create the
   viral moment yourself.
2. **Test concepts before building them.** Pushscroll posted faked demo videos before
   writing code. Run 5–10 concept videos per idea, and build only what clears a bar you
   set beforehand: say 300K+ views and "what app is this" comments.
3. **Speed matters more than polish for a second app.** Clones arrive in 1–6 months. A
   2–3 week build captures the window. Locturne's engine (quiz onboarding, paywall,
   Screen Time, AlarmKit) is what makes that speed possible.
4. **Retention is the only thing that holds the position after the clones arrive.**
   Every red-team found followers stalling while leaders kept their ratings and rank.
   Your known weak spot is the deciding factor here.

## 5. What this means for Locturne

- **Don't pivot to anything on this list.** Every alternative scored worse than
  finishing Locturne.
- **Locturne is under the same rule.** Erly, Wayk and Awake already exist, so it wins
  only through:
  - a mechanic that's clearly different ("your apps stay asleep until you get up")
  - your videos
  - retention
- **The chart tooling is reusable.** `market-scan/tools/` can pull every category chart
  in minutes and scan any niche's saturation. Run it weekly and diff the results, and
  you'll see new niches the week they first chart, not months later. Keep scans slow:
  five agents at once got this connection rate-limited.

## 6. Caveats

- Apple rate-limited the iTunes Search API partway through. Later scans used a
  web-search fallback that sees about 12–90 apps per niche, so competitor counts are
  minimums. That makes the "flooded" verdicts conservative.
- The web-search budget was used up, so there is no third-party revenue data (Sensor
  Tower, Appfigures). Revenue figures are estimates from chart rank.
- A few points are from the agents' memory and weren't re-checked:
  - Take-Two's takedown history
  - FAA test volumes
  - App Store age-law status
  - Apple's alcohol rules
