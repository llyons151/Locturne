# 06 · Activation, the aha moment, and the first days

Cross-episode synthesis of Sub Club batches 01–10. Citation format `B03 · 2023-10-18`. "(?)" = garbled-caption number. Tags: **[Hard]** / **[Freemium]** / **[Both]**.

## Consensus

1. **Activation is a count of a repeated core action, and it can be measured and designed toward.** [Both]
   - Aperture client (content app): 6 stories = "hard activated"; users who hit 6 before the trial converted trial→paid 2x better; after story 6, each next story kept ~98% (observed; B03 · 2023-10-18). They gave 10 free because 6 was an average.
   - Ladder: 3 workouts/week in week 1 makes week 2 "exponentially" likelier and predicts month-1 retention; 1–2 trial workouts → "very high" likelihood to pay (observed; B06 · 2025-03-09). Target lowered from 4 to 3 (B09 · 2026-03-07).
   - Zumba: aha = 3 classes; onboarding sets a weekly goal defaulted to 3, home counter toward 3, early messages push to class 3 (B07 · 2025-08-06).
   - Petit: the main driver of first payment → first renewal is ~3 completions of the core activity (practitioner claim; B02 · 2022-03-02).
   - Tolan: first *long* conversation (B06 · 2025-04-02). Life360: creating/joining a circle + inviting in the first 7 days correlates with D7, which correlates with 1-year retention (observed; B10 · 2026-05-13). Reading.com: reading the first story book (arrives at lesson 10 — they want it in lesson 1) (B07 · 2025-05-28).

2. **The first session and first ~30 days set the habit; after that it's very hard to change behaviour.** [Both]
   - Skylight: family patterns set within ~30 days; features set up in onboarding keep being used; new features after that are hard to adopt (observed; B09 · 2026-02-18).
   - Baxter: most cancellations happen in the first two months; optimise onboarding so new users adopt steady users' habits (observed across clients; B01 · 2021-12-13).
   - Layfield: the trial decision is session 0, ~first 10 minutes; recovery channels are weak (good email ≈ 30% opens, ~2% net CTR) (B07 · 2025-04-16).
   - Duolingo: even 1–2 days away makes coming back significantly harder (B06 · 2025-03-06).

3. **Deliver the core value inside onboarding — show, don't tell.** [Both]
   - PhotoRoom: users dropped before seeing background removal; moved it to within 3–4 taps (qualitative tests; B01 · 2021-09-27).
   - Rise: personal sleep debt + schedule within ~2 min (B03). Calm: rain sounds from first open (B08). Duolingo: a lesson inside onboarding (B03). Welltory: pulls user's own data and gives insight during onboarding (B07 · 2025-05-02).
   - Natal: human-reviewed posture assessment → 68–70% of finishers complete a first workout (observed; B10 · 2026-05-27). Simply Draw: AI animates the kid's drawing in FTUE — aha aimed at the paying parent; "really increased" conversion (experiment, no number; B10 · 2026-06-24).
   - Daphne Tideman: give the smallest possible feeling of progress in session one (a first practice, a "3-day challenge", "profile X% complete"); for goals that can't happen now, get a reminder set or step 1 done (opinion; B07).

4. **Make the commitment happen automatically tomorrow.** [Both — directly relevant to scheduled blocking]
   - Opal: onboarding into a scheduled blocking session was a "huge win" — user picks apps, app creates Mon–Fri 9–5 by default, next day blocking starts without the user remembering; editing easy but default automatic (A/B, size not given; B04 · 2024-01-10).
   - Batch-04 synthesis: "the best onboarding ends in something that happens tomorrow without the user remembering it."

5. **If the value is invisible (it happens in the background), surface it.** [Both]
   - RoboKiller: users pay *not* to interact; the team had to build reports/notifications showing what was blocked (anecdote; B06 · 2024-12-11). Life360: people pay for peace of mind only if the product keeps it top of mind (B07 · 2025-05-14). Skylight: don't label premium features so renewers judge total value (B09).

6. **Teach in the moment, not with FAQs or push.** [Both]
   - Ladder: coach voiceover in a "welcome workout" telling you to open the journal → ~70–75% now log workouts; in-the-moment instruction "10x more effective" (opinion from observation; B09 · 2026-03-07). Aperture: find the exact drop step (starting story 2) and remove friction there — autoplay, tooltips, rewards (B03).

## Contested

**A. Activate before the paywall, or paywall first?**
- Aperture kept the trial offer early despite activated users converting 2x (B03). Morris (Tinder): don't take a card before a successful outcome (B08 · 2026-01-07). Ladder: "use the product to convince", offers segmented by trial workouts (B06). Zumba: hard paywall before product won; then one free class, then paywall on close (B07). → In practice: paywall early, then design the trial period around hitting the activation count. **[Hard]**

**B. Onboarding completion time as a signal.** Petit: fast finishers were trial-and-cancel in one app, best users in another — not universal (B07 · 2025-08-21).

**C. "Wow" ≠ retention.** Mojo's earlier AR app had a big wow, Apple featuring, hundreds of thousands of downloads and no retention (anecdote; B05 · 2024-07-10). ElevenLabs: shareable moments must match value the user is proud of (B09). Coconote: a 41M-view "toy" video drove almost no revenue (B09).

## Other first-days notes
- ~30% of "true" installs never open the app in one product — check before blaming onboarding (observed; Aperture B03).
- Life360 day-2 personalised nudge naming a circle member ("What was John up to yesterday?") (B10).
- Opal: most day-1 re-engagement attempts after onboarding failed (A/B; B04) — except trial-cancel 50% offer (theme 08).
- Early proxies for new features: logging on day 1–2, week 2–4 retention (Ladder, B09).
- Early onboarding can be rough while testing PMF; a polished onboarding over a weak product gives false signal (Jacob, B03 · 2023-12-13).
