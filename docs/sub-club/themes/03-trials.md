# 03 · Free trials: length, card, removal, reminders, reverse trials

Cross-episode synthesis of Sub Club batches 01–10. Citation format `B09 · 2026-03-02`. "(?)" = garbled-caption number. Tags: **[Hard]** / **[Freemium]** / **[Both]**. For trial-timeline paywall design see theme 02; for trial-cancel save offers see theme 08.

## Consensus

1. **Offering a trial in the first session drives volume; keep it early even if warmer users convert better later.** [Both]
   - Hannah Parvaz: activated users (6 stories) converted 2x better, but the onboarding trial offer's volume was so much larger it was never removed; re-offer later instead (test; B03 · 2023-10-18).
   - Duolingo: trials helped even in a freemium app where paid features don't need "trying" — the CPO resisted and was wrong (A/B; B08 · 2026-01-21).
   - Mojo: trial at end of onboarding generates most trials; the trial answered "they haven't tried the app yet" (B05 · 2024-07-10).

2. **7 days is the default sweet spot; long trials (14–30 days) mostly lose on net and slow learning.** [Both]
   - Zumba: 7-day beat 14/30-day on trial-to-paid — more urgency and engagement (A/B; B07 · 2025-08-06).
   - Duolingo: 14 → 7 days (plus more trials per user) gave more net conversions and doubled experiment velocity (A/B; B09 · 2026-03-02).
   - Marcus Burke: 3-day only speeds up reading results; if 7-day converts 5–10% better, keep 7 (opinion; B04 · 2024-04-04). Petit: shortening 7 → 3 days so ad platforms "see" conversions doesn't help (B07 · 2025-08-21).
   - Greg (plant app): 30-day trial "too much for a mobile app", stretching payback to ~90 days (B01 · 2021-07-14).
   - Opal ran 7-day and read every test in ~8 days (B04 · 2024-01-10). Most guests with a hard paywall use 7 (Opal, Zumba annual, BoldVoice, Ladder, Burner option).

3. **Trial length should match how long the value takes to appear.** [Both]
   - Nomorobo chose 14 days because a blocked robocall may not happen in the first days (B03 · 2023-11-01). Maple Media's calendar app uses 7 days so users see a full week; the same approach fails for a weather app used for one storm (B02 · 2022-03-16). Captions (instant-value AI video) found 3-day beat 7-day (A/B; B05 · 2024-10-16).

4. **Very few people just "forget to cancel"; trial-to-paid is a real signal of value.** [Both]
   - Barnard: single-digit trial-to-paid means no PMF; 10–20%+ means real value; some apps convert only ~5% even of 3-day trials (B03 · 2023-08-23).
   - SOSA 2026: 55% of all 3-day-trial cancellations happen on day 0 and rising (B09 · 2026-03-06). Duolingo tracks day-0 cancel as an intent signal; cancelled trials rarely un-cancel (B09 · 2026-03-02).
   - Benchmarks (see table): median ~31% (RevenueCat via host, B10); 30–50% typical (Marcus Burke, B04); warm/organic audiences 68–90% (None to Run, Natal, Shot Pattern).

5. **Trial transparency and reminders raise conversion rather than lowering it.** [Both] See theme 02 #1 (None to Run, Opal, Duolingo, Built With Science).

## Contested

**A. Remove the trial from the monthly plan?**
- *Won:* Zumba — $14.99/mo no trial, $99/yr with 7-day trial; annual share ~30% → ~60%, annual trial-to-paid 30s% → ~56% (mixed changes; B07 · 2025-08-06). Natal copied it: monthly subs +~2,000%, quarterly +46%, annual +21% (experiment, now permanent; B10 · 2026-05-27).
- *Lost:* Reading.com — removing the monthly trial pushed more to annual but realized LTV fell 17%; hesitant monthly users were lost entirely (A/B; B07 · 2025-05-28). Built With Science — first "pay now for 20% off" toggle lost even with warm traffic because copy framed the trial as the worse deal (A/B; B10).
- Context: Natal is a very warm, founder-led, $25/mo audience; Reading.com is parents at $12.49/mo. **Test, don't copy.**

**B. Card up front or not?**
- *No card:* Ladder has never required one; users who do 1–2 workouts are "very likely" to pay (observed; B03, B06 · 2025-03-09).
- *No card failed:* Astropad's 14-day no-card web trial got huge downloads but low paid conversion; $30 upfront App Store purchase raised revenue "way up" (observed; B01 · 2022-01-05). Skylight tested trials with and without card — neither lifted conversion for a considered hardware purchase (A/B; B09 · 2026-02-18).

**C. Free trial vs paid trial / discounted first period / money-back.**
- Rise (web): a heavily discounted first period beats free trials because it trains the ad algorithm away from instant cancellers (A/B, no numbers; B09 · 2026-03-05). Reading.com (web): 30-day money-back guarantee instead of trial — lower conversion but better buyers and few refunds (observed; B07). Duolingo: China uses ~$0.99 paid trials; host says $1 trials send a stronger day-1 ad signal (B09 · 2026-03-02). Nathan Hudson: a $1 intro screened out fake cards (anecdote; B06 · 2025-01-08). Ladder: $5 first month retained ~half into month 2 at full price (test; B06 · 2025-03-09).
- Ravi Mehta's framework: free-to-play if network effects; ~7-day trial if habit loop forms fast; pay upfront, no trial, if financial commitment drives use — "you'd never give a gym a 7-day free trial" (framework; B08 · 2025-11-26).
- Mostly web-only evidence; in-app free trials remain the default.

**D. Long trials in aggregate data.**
- SOSA 2026: trials of 17+ days convert 42.5% vs 25.5% for short ones, yet apps moved to 3-day. Hosts call it selection bias (B09 · 2026-03-06). Captions 3-day > 7-day vs RevenueCat report saying 7-day does better (B05).

**E. Reverse trials / "free taste".**
- Works: Duolingo 3-day no-card, no-auto-renew "free taste" framed as a reward after a lesson, with a "your benefits have ended" drawer (observed as effective; B09 · 2026-03-02). BoldVoice: dismissers still get the full assessment and day 1 of the plan, then must start a 7-day trial from day 2 (B10 · 2026-09-16). Flighty: first flight free with all pro features (B08).
- Little success: Barnard's 7-day non-renewing reverse trial shown when a user dismisses Apple's payment sheet (B07 · 2025-04-16). Host: reverse trials are hard to communicate because users fear auto-charge (B09).

**F. Trial for considered/hardware purchases.** Skylight: intent is set up front; trial takers were the same people who'd subscribe anyway (B09). Visible has no trial (hardware) and de-risks with a free app instead (B10 · 2026-08-19).

## Repeat trials
- Duolingo gives multiple trials across lifecycle stages (needs separate SKUs/offers on iOS) (B09 · 2026-03-02). LinkedIn tests a second free trial after 12 months ("the product has changed") (B08 · 2025-09-17). Quizlet re-trials only after ~a year plus signs of return (B06 · 2025-03-08). **[Freemium mostly]**
