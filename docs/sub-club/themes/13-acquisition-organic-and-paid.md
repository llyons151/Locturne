# 13 · Acquisition: organic video, creators, paid UA, traffic quality

Added theme because many episodes are about acquisition and the founder plans short-form video promotion. Kept brief; most ad-ops detail is omitted. Citation format `B05 · 2024-11-13`. "(?)" = garbled-caption number.

## Consensus

1. **Traffic source sets what converts; warm/high-intent traffic flatters every metric.** [Both]
   - Press traffic converts and retains poorly (Weather Up B04; Burner "Vogue mention" B08; Barnard: featuring drags ARPU B03). High-intent search inflates it (B03). Warm communities give 68–90% trial→paid (None to Run B02, Natal B10, Shot Pattern "juiced" B07). Built With Science: organic YouTube 35–40% vs Meta ~25% trial→paid; clickbait ads → cheap trials that don't convert (B10). TikTok traffic lower intent at BoldVoice (B10). Under-25s trial-and-cancel (Marcus Burke B04; Petit B07). Early adopters outperform later users on every metric (Phil Carter B06).

2. **Short-form organic video can work from zero followers — framed as story/problem, not an ad.** [Both]
   - ~95% of For You watch time goes to non-followed accounts; plug after 50% watch time; listicle/POV formats ("top 5 apps", "5 controversial rules at our wedding") (Joseph Choi B05 · 2024-11-13, B08 · 2025-10-28; clip B08 · 2025-10-14). Waitlist validation: Breezy ~5,000 signups from one 15-second video (B05, B08).
   - Frame as problem/identity, not toy: Coconote's 41M-view "brain-rot" video drove little revenue (B09, B10). 1SE duet → ~1M views, #1 in App Store; "What app is that?" comments signal success (B01 · 2021-08-11). "Ugly wins"; best Monkey Taps ad was a notification screenshot (Petit B03). Quick screen recording with CapCut captions beat $750–1,500 creator videos (Shot Pattern B07).
   - Opal's best ads: mission-led, don't show product; licensed a user's viral film (B04, B09).

3. **Small creator stables beat agencies and big influencers.** [Both]
   - Creators <50k followers with 1–2 hits, daily posts on a branded account, $500–3,000/mo + ~$1 CPM (Choi B05). Tolan: ~1 in 20 videos takes off (B06). Coconote: ~30 creators in 2 months, 3 mattered; 5–10k followers with Gmail in bio; agency creators "take more than they give"; no paid ads to $6.7M ARR (B09, B10). Speechify: ~1 in 300 creatives takes off (B02).

4. **Paid UA only on top of proven conversion; minimum budgets are real.** [Hard especially]
   - Petit: ~10–20 optimisation events/day per campaign ≈ $10–20k/mo per channel; don't start below $10k/mo; <$5–10k go to Apple Search Ads and micro-influencers (B03 · 2023-08-07). Hannah Parvaz: ~$6k/mo minimum at ~$5 CPI (B03). Built With Science waited a year after launch before ads (B10). Opal ran profitably on paid from year 1 at 100%+ D8 ROAS (B04).
   - Map trial events honestly (trial as "Trial" not "Purchase") — ~35% lower cost per trial (Alper Taner B08, B10). Send qualified-trial events filtered by early activity (Petit B07).

5. **Ask "where did you hear about us?" in onboarding.** Opal (most reliable attribution, B04), Marcus Burke (B04), Genius Scan (B06), MySwimPro (B04), Babbel (B05).

## Other single-source notes
- Seasonality: New Year peak for habit apps (1SE B01, HabitKit B05); ad costs cheapest Dec 15–late Jan ("Q5"), November expensive (Hannah Parvaz B03).
- Share cards/outputs people brag about spread apps (Slopes 3D run card B02; Rapchat auto-video 10x B01; Mojo animated stories B05; Ladder widget as billboard B09). Copying "Spotify Wrapped" rarely works; shareable moments must reflect value users are proud of (ElevenLabs B09).
- App Store: new designed screenshots +10% conversion (None to Run B02); modernised screenshots lost ~80% of tests for a #1 app (Super Unlimited B09); first screenshot in customers' words (Coconote B09); ask for ratings after first success, not after 10 uses (Steve Young B03).
- Keep any single creative ≤30% of spend (Pray.com B05; Paired/Blinkist B02).
