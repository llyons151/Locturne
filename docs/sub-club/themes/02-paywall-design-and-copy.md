# 02 · Paywall design and copy

Cross-episode synthesis of Sub Club batches 01–10. Citation format `B02 · 2022-06-15`. "(?)" = garbled-caption number. Tags: **[Hard]** / **[Freemium]** / **[Both]**. Ranked strongest evidence first.

## Consensus

1. **The Blinkist-style trial-timeline paywall (what happens on which day + "we'll remind you before you're charged") is the most replicated paywall win in the podcast.** [Both]
   - None to Run: +23–25% trial starts, no significant drop in trial-to-paid, matching Blinkist's own ~23% (A/B; B02 · 2022-06-15).
   - Opal: "dozens of challengers" never beat it; once placement was right, content was not a big lever (A/B; B04 · 2024-01-10).
   - Duolingo: a timeline at checkout ("today is day 5, we'll notify you") beats subscription aversion, strongest outside the US (A/B; B08 · 2026-01-21). Letting users *choose* the reminder day raised trial conversion further (A/B; B09 · 2026-03-02).
   - Built With Science: annual-first + timeline (Day 1 plan, Day 7 adapts, Day 12 reminder) moved annual share ~60% → 75–85% with no price change (A/B; B10 · 2026-09-02).
   - Also adopted by Quizlet (B03 · 2023-12-13); Petit says it spread in 2021 (B02 · 2022-03-02); Jacob calls it brand-positive (B09 · 2026-03-06).
   - Duolingo's framing: "the goal is not to persuade, it's to give clarity and confidence" (B09 · 2026-03-02).

2. **Lead with annual; hide monthly behind "view all plans".** [Both]
   - None to Run 56% → 76% yearly (with quick-trial card; B02). Mojo +15–20pp annual adoption (A/B; B09 · 2026-03-01). Built With Science 60% → 75–85% (A/B; B10). Genius Scan: "nearly everyone" buys annual after hiding monthly (observed; B06 · 2025-01-22). Ladder: simply adding annual side-by-side took annual from ~0% to ~20%, then ~30% at 50% off monthly (observed; B03 · 2023-11-29).

3. **Show the annual price as a per-month equivalent.** [Both]
   - Mojo: ~+10% revenue US, ~+30–40% Brazil/Mexico (A/B; B09 · 2026-03-01). Microsoft 365: per-month display won (A/B, no figures; B04 · 2024-05-15). Phil Carter lists it as standard design (B03).

4. **CTA copy must name the trial/zero cost; generic "Subscribe"/"Buy now" loses.** [Both]
   - Duolingo ranking: "Try for $0.00" > "Try for free" > "Start (my) free trial" > "Subscribe"; $0.00 lifted *both* trial starts and trial-to-paid (A/B, direction only; B08 · 2026-01-21, B10 · 2026-04-30).
   - Microsoft: "Buy now" under a trial headline scares users; "Try free for 1 month" won (A/B; B04 · 2024-05-15).
   - Zumba: "Start today" beat alternatives (A/B; B07 · 2025-08-06). Superwall: imperative instructions ("Press the purple button to start your free trial") (opinion; B02 · 2022-06-29).
   - SOSA 2026: "Continue" is the most common CTA (B09) — but Life360 names "Continue" instead of "Subscribe" as one of the stacked dark patterns (B07 · 2025-05-14).

5. **Fewer choices convert better.** [Both]
   - Burner: 1 prepaid product vs 5 "drastically outperformed" (A/B; B02 · 2022-07-13). Genius Scan: removing the $0.99 tier kept ~100 purchases/day, all moving to the $3 tier (observed; B06). Built With Science: *adding* explanatory context to the pricing page lowered conversion (A/B; B10). UDocs single tier +12% conversion (B03). ElevenLabs: one price on mobile (B09 · 2026-02-04). Phil Carter: early apps offer one plan (B03). Tinder: too many tiers caused paralysis (B09 · 2026-03-04).

6. **Defaults and anchors swing results massively.** [Both]
   - Weather Up: monthly default → ~10% more subscribers (later quoted as 20% (?)) but ~60% lower first-month realized LTV; ~33% switched away from an annual default vs ~8% from monthly (A/B, significant; B04 · 2024-03-20).
   - Microsoft: defaulting the pricier Family plan raised sales of both plans (A/B; B04 · 2024-05-15).
   - Skylight: subscription on by default at checkout, "most people don't" opt out (observed; B09 · 2026-02-18).

7. **The screens before the paywall are part of the paywall.** [Hard especially]
   - Sylvain Gauchet (B06 · 2025-03-14), Natal host (B10 · 2026-06-08), Citizen's narrative onboarding with two paywall moments (B02 · 2022-06-29), Opal's projection-then-offer (B04). See theme 05.

8. **Sell outcomes/emotion; users don't read.** [Both]
   - Superwall: "feel your strongest" beats "AI weightlifting coach"; mine phrasing from "why do you love X" surveys (B02). Shawn Gong: users decide emotionally in ~1 second (B09 · 2026-03-04). Duolingo: "people don't read" (B08). Coconote: customer phrase "never miss a key detail" used verbatim (B09). Calm's paywall is tuned to someone awake at 1am (soft blue, calm language) (B03 · 2023-12-13).

## Contested

**A. One plan vs three tiers.**
- *Three:* Shawn Gong (Tinder/Grindr) — three turns "buy or not?" into "which one?" (opinion); Ravi Mehta — 2–3 tiers max plus consumables (B08 · 2025-11-26); Life360 showed platinum to high-propensity users, doubling platinum share with no gold loss (experiment; B10 · 2026-05-13); Microsoft decoy effect (B04).
- *One:* Phil Carter, Burner, ElevenLabs, Genius Scan (above); Condé Nast: new premium tiers get low single-digit adoption (B08 · 2025-10-29); Mojo avoids tiers for complexity (B05); Codecademy passed $50M ARR before a second tier (B07 · 2025-04-16).
- Context: tiers pay off at scale or with a whale segment; early and small apps win with one plan. **For a new hard-paywall app: one plan (annual + monthly fallback).**

**B. Paywall video.**
- FitnessAI +80% with video (confounded with placement move) vs Citizen video test flat (B02 · 2022-06-29). Weather Up used a feature video (no test) (B04). Mojo's US winner used background video; Japan wanted long scroll (B09).

**C. Long vs short paywall.**
- Mimo multi-screen paywall +60% trial opt-in vs single screen (test; B06 · 2025-03-14). Japan: long, social-proof-heavy scroll won (+20% revenue at Mojo; "skyrocketed" at Simply) but the same failed in the US where a clean paywall won (A/B; B09 · 2026-03-01, B10 · 2026-06-24). Built With Science: more content at purchase hurt (B10). → Split *across screens* can work; *dense single* paywalls lose in the US.

**D. Trial toggles / optional trial.**
- Burner: making the trial optional instead of default was "very positive" (A/B; B08 · 2025-12-10). Yousician host: toggle gave big lifts but Apple is rejecting it and 6–12-month cohorts unverified (B09 · 2026-02-28). Built With Science: pay-now-for-20%-off + money-back vs 14-day trial lost first, then a version framing both as good showed "very strong lift" (A/B in progress; B10). Sylvain: stacking toggles, wheels and backup offers feels scammy and raises churn (B06).

**E. "$0.00" and "Continue" — clarity or dark pattern?**
- Duolingo reports lift; Yuliya Lennox's host questions whether "$0.00" is clear about auto-renew (B10 · 2026-07-22). Layfield's host: hiding price near CTA raises taps but more users back out at Apple's sheet (B07 · 2025-04-16).

**F. Colour.**
- Maple Media: teal was a notably bad paywall colour (A/B, no number; B02 · 2022-03-16). Superwall: colour doesn't matter; the CTA being the *only* coloured element with high contrast does (opinion; B02).

## Other design tactics (single-source)
- Personalised paywall per entry point/intent: LinkedIn one intent question (B08 · 2025-09-17); Calm celebrity sleep-story paywall (B03); affiliate traffic sees creator's face and a 1-month trial (B10 · 2026-09-22); traffic-source-specific offers (Weather Up, B04).
- "Redeem your free week" quick-trial card straight to Apple sheet: ~100 subs in 2 weeks (early; B02).
- Paywall craft pays: Tinder's shimmer animation; "many paywalls look designed by a PM" (B08 · 2026-01-07). Duolingo celebrates purchase (Duo astronaut) (B03).
- Social proof on paywall: reviews from someone in the target demo (Marcus Burke, B04 · 2024-04-04); objection-specific proof (theme 09).
- "24/7 support" on paywall — untested hypothesis (Captions, B05 · 2024-10-16).
- App icon change raised paywall views 57% (anecdote; B03 · 2023-08-23).
