# Name research: is "Trundle" a good name?

Researched September 25, 2026. There were three tracks: legal conflicts and
availability, meaning and perception, and benchmark alternatives. This builds on
the earlier naming table in [IDEA_SCORECARD.md](IDEA_SCORECARD.md), and the name
was locked on September 24 in [BRAND_PRINCIPLES.md](BRAND_PRINCIPLES.md).

## Verdict

**Trundle is usable but not strong: about 6/10.** Nothing blocks it legally.
Its weaknesses are search and distinctiveness, not law. None of the vetted
alternatives clearly beats it, though, so this is a "keep it unless something
clearly better turns up" result, not a "rename" result.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can we legally use it? | Probably yes. No live US TRUNDLE mark covers software (class 9 or 42) or health (class 44). Riot has never filed on "Trundle". |
| Can people find it? | In the App Store, yes: the only same-name iOS app is a travel app with 0 ratings. On YouTube, TikTok and Google, not by the bare word: League of Legends owns those results. |
| Does it fit the product? | Half. The pun works once explained (you trundle 200 steps, and a trundle bed means sleep). Cold, it says "slow and heavy", not "sleep, phone, or get up". |
| Does it fit the raccoon? | Yes. Raccoons waddle. Soft, heavy sounds suit a tired mascot. |
| Does it fit "sassy"? | No. It sounds cozy and dopey, so the voice has to carry all the sass. |
| Can we own the web presence? | Poorly. trundle.com is for sale at $150K, and .app, .io and .co are held. The exact handles are squatted. |

## 1. Legal and availability

**App stores (low risk)**
- **iOS:** the iTunes Search API (US, GB, AU) finds only
  [Trundle-Travel](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trundle-travel/id6753061680) (Tabibito,
  India, 0 ratings).
- **Google Play:**
  - The same travel app, with 50+ downloads.
  - "Trundle Bed Ideas", a furniture app.
  - "trundl – Steps for Charity", a UK walking app, now dormant.
- Nothing in Health, Productivity or sleep.

**US trademarks (low to medium risk).** A direct USPTO search for `trundl*` found:
- **Live marks:**
  - TRUNDLE, jewelry (class 14)
  - HOTEL TRUNDLE, hotels (class 43)
  - TRUNDLEBED TALES, education (class 41)
  - TRUNDLIA, earphones (class 9) and class 11
- **TRUNDL** (Trundl Inc., class 42, software development): the nearest conflict.
  It's a B2B Atlassian partner, not a consumer app, but an examiner could still cite it.
- **Dead marks:** an old TRUNDLE for auto wheels, plus expired bed marks.
- **Nothing live named TRUNDLE covers classes 9, 42 or 44.**

**Riot Games (low risk legally, medium in marketing)**
- Of Riot's 366 USPTO filings, only a handful are champion names: Ahri, Jinx,
  Teemo, Yasuo and others. **None is for Trundle.**
- Every Riot enforcement case found (Riot.im → Element, Riot Lacrosse) was over
  the RIOT house mark, not a champion name.
- Riot's fan policy bars using character names as handles and hashtags. So: never use
  #leagueoflegends or #trundle tags, and never make LoL-meme marketing.

**EU and UK trademarks: not verified.** TMview and UK IPO blocked automated search.
Check manually at https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/ and https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/.

## 2. Search: the real problem

- **YouTube "trundle":** the top 19 of 19 results are League gameplay and guides.
- **TikTok "trundle":** dominated by LoL clips, plus some trundle-bed furniture.
- **Google page 1:** about 5 of 10 results are LoL (the official page, the wiki,
  Mobalytics, OP.GG, U.gg). The rest are dictionaries, Wikipedia and Amazon trundle
  beds.
- **The champion itself:** about a 2.7% pick rate. He's mid-popularity, but has a
  lasting meme identity as the "troll king".
- **The Netflix villain:** a second troll king named Trundle is the villain of
  *Hilda and the Mountain King* (Netflix, 2021).

**Why this matters more for us than for most apps.** Growth is short-form video,
and the audience overlaps gaming. Someone who hears "Trundle" in a video and
searches TikTok or YouTube lands on League, not on us. The App Store works fine.
The fixes:
- always say and show **"Trundle app"** or the full tagline;
- keep the raccoon on screen, so the character is what people recognise and remember.

## 3. Meaning and perception

**Meanings**
- The verb means to move slowly and heavily on wheels. The noun means a small wheel,
  or a trundle bed.
- The verb comes from Old English *trendel* ("ring, disk"). "Trundle-bed" dates from
  the 1540s, so sleep is built into the word.

**US vs UK** (Google Ngram, 2018–22)
- The verb reads British: UK use is about 2x US.
- "Trundle bed" is the American term, about 2.6x more common in the US; the UK says
  "truckle bed".
- So **US 18–34s hear the bed first**, a sleepover/childhood association.
- UK and AU listeners hear the slow-rolling verb first.

**Slang to know about**
- **It rhymes with "grundle"** (US slang for the perineum). Expect the joke in
  comments. A sassy brand can survive it, or even use it.
- Urban Dictionary has minor sexual and "heavy person barging through" senses. None
  has traction, but avoid body-size jokes in copy.

**Sound symbolism**
- The /ʌ/ is a back vowel, and "-ndle" is a soft, round ending.
- Klink (2000) and Yorkston & Menon (2004) link back vowels to **big, heavy, slow,
  soft**. That fits bedtime and a tired raccoon. It fits "get up now" and sassy less
  well.
- These effects replicate only partly, so don't over-weight them.
- The plosive "T" start helps recall (Vanden Bergh et al., 1984).

**Spelling**
- It's pronounced one way only.
- The weak spot is the "-dle" ending: listeners who don't know the word may write
  "Trundel" or "Trundal".

**Other characters named Trundle**
- The LoL troll king.
- The Hilda/Netflix troll king.
- Trundle the hedgehog (*The Six Crowns* children's books), a cute-animal precedent.
- A 2023 goblin short film.
- Minor "Mr. Trundle" TV roles.
- None in Thomas & Friends or Ben & Holly.

**How comparable apps are named.** The winners in this space are 1–2 syllable real
words or simple compounds, mostly evocative rather than descriptive: Opal, Finch,
Forest, Brick, Calm, Rise, one sec. Naming the app after the mascot is a proven
pattern: Finch's pet is the app, and Duolingo's owl Duo became its famous sassy
TikTok face. Trundle fits that pattern.

## 4. Alternatives benchmarked

About 40 names were brainstormed. Most failed on App Store collisions:
- **Direct competitors:** Kip (an app blocker already exists) and Shush (a screen-time
  app already exists).
- **Registered marks:** ShutEye.
- **Crowded:** Lull, Nod, Snoozy, Wakey, Drowsy, Pawse, Snug, Waddle.
- **Already a raccoon app:** Scraps.

The shortlist:

| Name | Fit | Conflicts | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bleary** | Sleep-blurred eyes: instantly "tired" | No app, trademark or game character found | Low (not USPTO-cleared) |
| **Mopey** | A Duo-style character name | Minor cartoon characters and mope.io. "Mopey" means sad, which may clash with "never shaming" | Low–med |
| **Shoo** | Shooing someone out of bed; echoes "Shh." | Clean, but sounds like "shoe", so it fails the spell-after-hearing test | Med |
| Rascal | The strongest raccoon link (*Rascal* memoir and anime) | Generic word; a kids' app; RASCAL powerchairs | Med |
| Trudge | Walking wearily | A monster in R.E.P.O. (a 2025 hit) and a Yu-Gi-Oh character, the same gamer problem | Med–high |
| Budge, Conk, Otis, Smudge, Plod, Mosey, Lids | — | Crowded or conflicting (Conker, the Dead Rising meme Otis, Mosey Mate step app, Lids hats) | Med–high |

**How they compare to Trundle**
- Bleary is the only candidate cleaner than Trundle on search and conflicts.
- Bleary is an adjective, not a character name.
- Bleary is no sassier than Trundle.
- It has not had a USPTO check, a domain check or a handle check.
- Mopey is the best character name, but its tone is risky.

**Raccoon notes**
- Never use anything containing "coon", which is a slur.
- Avoid the famous game and film raccoons: Sly Cooper, Rocket, Tom Nook/Tanooki,
  Meeko, Rigby.
- "Trash panda", the "masked bandit" and being nocturnal (on a "night shift")
  are usable jokes.

## Recommendation

1. **Keep Trundle** unless a video test says otherwise. It's legally clear in the
   US, it's free in the App Store, and it suits the mascot. The weaknesses (search,
   domains, tone) can be managed with a tagline and the raccoon. None of the
   alternatives is clearly better.
2. **If you're still open to changing it, test before committing.** Post two
   concept videos with the same script, one with Trundle and one with Bleary. Compare
   comments that repeat the name, profile taps, and whether viewers can find it.
   Renaming is cheap now, before launch, and expensive later.
3. **If keeping Trundle, do this now:**
   - Grab **usetrundle.com** and **trundlesleep.com**, plus consistent handles
     (**@trundle.app** on TikTok is free).
   - Confirm whether TikTok **@trundleapp** is yours.
   - Run a manual EUIPO/UKIPO check.
   - File a US intent-to-use application in class 9 (and 42/44 if the budget allows).
   - Use the App Store name "Trundle: Apps Sleep Till You're Up", or similar, so
     the store listing describes the app.
   - Never use LoL hashtags or memes.
   - Have a line ready for the "grundle" joke.
