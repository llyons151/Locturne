# TikTok and Instagram slideshows (research, 2026-10-05)

The question: slideshows (TikTok photo posts, Instagram carousels) are said to be one of
the main ways apps grow right now. Is it real, and should Locturne try it? This extends
[AI_UGC_RESEARCH.md](AI_UGC_RESEARCH.md), which covers AI-image slideshows and the Larry
case, and doesn't change the plan in GAME_PLAN ("build first, then real-footage videos").

Grades: **V** verified (primary source or dataset with numbers), **S** secondhand
(press, vendor blog, someone's claim), **U** unverified.

## 1. Short version

- **It's real, but it's a hits game.** A few app founders got huge results from
  repeating one slideshow format at volume. The typical result is much smaller: one
  automated account got ~100 downloads from a month at 3 posts a day.
- **TikTok photo posts are getting worse on average; Instagram carousels are strong.**
  Photo posting on TikTok more than doubled while views per post fell (§2).
- **Copy the format, never the post.** Both platforms now suppress duplicated content.
- **For Locturne it's a cheap hook lab:** find the hooks that work in slideshows, then
  film them for real. Two accounts, both openly yours, from about November 1 (§7).

### The reel that prompted this
[daniel.margu, Oct 5 2026](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeHsxTFMC6O/): an ad for
Stratos (trystratos.ai), a tool that remakes someone else's slideshow for your app. No

- **Oasis:** "Highest radium waters", bottled waters ranked.
- **Kalshi:** "There is now a 34% chance Bitcoin hits $100K before the end of 2026."
- **MenuFit/Skini:** "Best Dunkin pumpkin spice swaps for fat loss", last slide

The tool itself (paste a link, rewrite the slides for your app) is about an hour's work,
but cloning a specific post is exactly what the originality rules suppress.

## 4. The playbook operators describe (secondhand)

- **Structure:** hook (a person + a conflict) → tension → the app → CTA "it's called
  Locturne". Larry: 6 slides. Stronger Mobile shows the app by slide 2–3.
- **Diagnosis (Larry):** lots of views but no installs → fix the CTA slide; few views
  but good installs → fix the hook.
- **Sound:** post as a draft, then add a trending sound by hand in the app. The API
  can't set music, and "the highest-return 2 minutes" is adding it.
- **Warm-up:** 3–14 days of normal scrolling on a new account before posting.
- **Cadence:** 1–5 posts a day per account. Consistency and volume are the shared rule.
- **Folklore, unsourced:** the white "Classic" TikTok font with black outline, Pinterest
  images, the app as item 4 or 5 of a listicle.

## 5. Formats that fit Locturne (ideas, not measured)

1. **"POV: it's 7:02am and Instagram is still asleep 🦝"**, Loc's voice on the slides,
   the downstairs or 200-step unlock as the payoff.
2. **Listicle, "5 things that fixed my mornings"** (phone out of the bedroom, sunlight…)
   with Locturne as #4, "(I built this one)". The disclosure is part of the hook.
3. **Founder story:** "my screen time was 9 hours, so I built an app that won't open
   TikTok until I walk downstairs", with real screenshots.
4. **Before/after screen time**, real screenshots only.
5. **Loc reaction slides:** Loc judging the viewer's 3am scrolling. Original art, so no
   originality flag, no AI label and no image-rights problem.
6. **Useful-first, Oasis/Kalshi style:** "What 3am scrolling does to your morning,
   ranked", from [NIGHT_PHONE_SCIENCE.md](NIGHT_PHONE_SCIENCE.md), app on the last slide.

## 6. Rules and risks

- **Disclose your own app.** The FTC requires owners to disclose their connection (the
  2017 CSGOLotto case). A faceless "girl who found this app" account run by the founder
  is exactly what's prohibited. On TikTok, use the "Your brand" disclosure toggle;
  undisclosed promotion loses For You eligibility after 24 hours, and the label itself
  reportedly doesn't hurt reach. [CreatorIQ](https://creatoriq.com/blog/tiktok-enforcement-branded-content-disclosures)
- **Multiple accounts are allowed; networks posting the same thing aren't.** Duplicate
  slides across accounts get matched and struck.
- **No Pinterest images.** The photographers own them. Use your own photos,
  screenshots and Loc art.
- **Realistic AI people need the AI label** ([AI_UGC_RESEARCH.md](AI_UGC_RESEARCH.md) §3).

## 7. Recommendation: a 4-week hook lab

Try it, cheaply, from **about November 1**, alongside the real-footage videos planned for
mid-November. Real app screenshots should exist by then.

- **Two accounts, both openly yours:** a founder account ("student building an app that
  puts my apps to sleep") and a Loc account. Warm each up for 5–7 days. Cross-post every
  TikTok to Instagram as a carousel with music.
- **One post a day per account** for 4 weeks (~56 posts), rotating formats 1, 2, 3, 5
  and 6, 5–6 slides each.
- **CTA before launch:** "launches in January, follow" plus a link-in-bio waitlist on
  locturne.com with UTM tags per account and format. If App Review passes early, an
  App Store pre-order lets "it's called Locturne" work in search.
- **Track per post:** views, saves, shares, profile visits, waitlist sign-ups per 1K
  views. Add "where did you hear about us" to onboarding for January.
- **Keep going** after ~30 posts per account if one post passes 50K views **and** the
  best format gets ~3+ sign-ups per 1K views. Then move that hook into real-footage
  video and post more often from launch.
- **Park it** if median views stay under ~500 and nothing passes 10K.
- **Cost:** $0–30 and about 30 minutes a day. Building a remake tool is optional; do the
  first 20 or so by hand in Canva or CapCut to learn what works first.
