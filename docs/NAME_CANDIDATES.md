# Name candidates: ~250 names with an open .com and .app

Generated September 25, 2026, as a follow-up to [NAME_RESEARCH.md](NAME_RESEARCH.md).
The brief was 100–300 names, each with the **exact .com and the exact .app
unregistered**.

## How this was checked

- **Pool.** About 2,100 candidates were brainstormed:
  - character names
  - sleep and wake puns
  - raccoon and "bandit" angles
  - invented words
  - words from other languages
  - phrases about the mechanic
- **Domains.** Each was checked against the live registry databases (RDAP):
  - `.com` at Verisign
  - `.app` at Google Registry

  A 404 from the registry means the name is not registered.
- **Result.** 689 names had both domains free. The full list is in
  [name-candidates-com-and-app-free.txt](name-candidates-com-and-app-free.txt).
  The ~250 below are the curated ones. Rejected names include:
  - awkward combinations
  - anything starting with "nappy" (a UK word for diaper, and a slur about hair in the US)
  - "woke" (politically loaded)
  - "nod" (opioid slang)
  - "groggy" (a direct competitor is called Groggy)
- **App Store.** Every curated name was run through the iTunes Search API. None of the
  ~250 below has an app with the same name in the US store.
- **Characters and slang.** The top ~55 were searched for famous game or anime
  characters, brands and slang.

**Caveats**
- **Premium prices.** "Unregistered" doesn't mean standard price. Registries sometimes
  mark short or dictionary-like names as premium. Check the price at checkout on
  Porkbun or Cloudflare before getting attached to a name.
- **Availability changes.** These were available on September 25. Anyone can register
  them at any time.
- **Trademarks.** Nothing here has been cleared with USPTO. Run a search on the final
  2–3 names.

hand, and **all 153 have both .com and .app free**. Only one of them clashes with an
existing app: Luna Steps, a photo app. The top names were then searched on the web.

**Moon names to avoid**

- **Luna Bandit / Lunabandit:** an enemy in Kingdom Hearts II. Gamers will know it.
- **Moony-anything:** "Moony" is Remus Lupin's nickname in Harry Potter, and a
  Sailor Moon fan term.
- **Moonie-anything:** "Moonies" is a slang term for members of the Unification Church.
- **Mooncoon:** it splits off the racial slur "coon" (see the chat reasoning). The
  .com is taken anyway.
- **Moonpaw:** a Warriors cats character, and the .com is taken.
- **Moonshine-anything:** reads as alcohol.
- Anything with "Moon Moon" may invite the "Moon Moon" dumb-wolf meme. It's harmless
  but worth knowing.

## Rejected, and why

- **Existing app or brand in the same space:**
  - Unsnooze and Unsnoozeable (alarm apps, one with a walking challenge)
  - Napoleon (a screen-time app)
  - Unbed (an alarm app with 733 ratings)
  - Unrot, No Rot, Rotless and Bedrot (all screen-time apps)
  - Nope (a screen-time blocker)
  - Upsy (an alarm app)
  - Snoozer ("Snoozers", 402 ratings)
  - Napper (a baby sleep app, 4.5K ratings)
- **Too close to a raccoon concept or a game character:**
  - Nap Bandit (a BarkBox sleeping-raccoon dog toy)
  - Mapache (Spanish for raccoon, and TikTok already uses "app de mapache" for BitePal, a raccoon-mascot app)
  - Snorlet and Snorlo (read as Pokémon's Snorlax)
  - Pudgeon (Viva Piñata)
  - Mortimer (The Sims)
  - Pudgebug (Pudge is a Dota 2 hero)
  - Lotor (a Voltron villain)
- **Bad slang:**
  - Nodded Off and any "nod" name (opioid slang)
  - Crankyo ("crank" is meth slang, and CrankO is a LoL pro player)
  - Nappy-anything
  - Woke-anything
- **Crowded or conflicting:**
  - Cranky, Crabby, Grumpy (Grumpy Cat) and Snarky
  - Scooch (a step-tracking app)
  - Mittens, Dozer, Toddle and Scruff (the gay dating app)
