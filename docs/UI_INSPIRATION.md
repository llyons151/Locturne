# UI inspiration list (2026-10-06)

Why: the Apps tab (and most of the tab screens) read as filler. These are the
references to study before redesigning them. All links checked live on 2026-10-06.

## Why the Apps tab reads cheap (diagnosis)

1. **Fake brand icons.** In the web preview, apps are drawn as glyphs on coloured squares. They
   read as knock-offs. On device, Screen Time draws the real icons, so judge on device.
2. **No point of view.** It is a Settings list: 13 rows that all look the same, and nothing
   says what happens tonight. Good apps put the *state* first ("Asleep at 11:30 · 13 apps")
   and keep the list secondary.
3. **The main action is buried.** "Add or remove apps" sits below the tab bar, clipped.
4. **The background looks unfinished.** A grey card sits on black, with the blue sky peeking
   out only at the bottom corners.
