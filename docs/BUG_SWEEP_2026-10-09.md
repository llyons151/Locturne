# Bug sweep, 2026-10-09 (overnight)

An automated loop on branch `bug-sweep/2026-10-09`, starting at `47e36a9`. Each sweep
covers the whole app with 8 area finders, using a different lens each time. Every finding
gets two adversarial verifiers before a fixer touches it. The loop stops after **3 sweeps

Earlier sweeps: [2026-10-03](BUG_SWEEP_2026-10-03.md), [2026-10-04](BUG_SWEEP_2026-10-04.md).

## Fixed

**Home, sleep and nap**
1. **Home promised "Your apps go to sleep at 11:00 PM" when nothing would sleep.**
   - **What happened:** the tab redesign (`0206478`) dropped Home's health note, See plans
     and `awakeLine`. A user with no subscription (lapsed and stood down, never bought), an
     Ask to Buy wait, or a night iOS refused to arm read "Bedtime in 8h. Your apps go to
     sleep at 11:00 PM". At 23:30 the unheld night read as day, so it counted down to
     tomorrow's bedtime instead.
   - **Fix:** by day (including a night `heldPhase` reads as day), Home's hero comes from a
     new pure `dayHero` (`features/home/awake-line.ts`). When `rollUpHealth` needs attention,
     its title and detail lead, and the button becomes "See plans" when subscribing is the
     fix. Otherwise, with no night lock scheduled, the body is `awakeLine` ("Nothing is
     scheduled to sleep") and no bedtime is named.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`dayHero`).
2. **The nap ruler offered 5 and 10 minutes, which iOS always refuses.**
   - **What happened:** the Sleep sheet's ruler (`f05fac1`) started at 5 minutes.
     DeviceActivity refuses windows under 15 minutes (`intervalTooShort`), so those naps
     always failed with "iOS wouldn't start the nap. Try again in a moment."
   - **Fix:** `NAP_MIN` now comes from `NAP_SHORTEST` (15) in `lib/screen-time.ts`, and
     `startNap` refuses anything shorter before asking iOS.
   - **Tests:** `screen-time.test.ts` (a nap under 15 minutes is refused).

**Money and onboarding**
3. **A purchase that finished after the paywall was closed never kept "Remind me".**
   - **What happened:** the paywall's X stays live while RevenueCat posts the receipt. Closing
     it then saved the setup (`exit()`), so when the purchase resolved `purchased` or
     `pending`, the late branches in `finishSetup` and `buy` skipped `saveTrialReminder`
     behind `!hasRoutine()`. `syncTrialEnd` only schedules when `TRIAL_REMINDER_KEY` is true,
     so no trial-end reminder and no Home notice came before the $59.99 charge. A See plans
     rerun always has a routine, so every late purchase there was hit.
   - **Fix:** the `!hasRoutine()` guard now covers only `saveSetup` (keeping later Routine
     edits). The reminder choice is always saved, in both late branches.
   - **Tests:** `features/onboarding/purchase-lifetime.test.ts` (late success and late Ask to
     Buy both save the reminder).

**Lock core**
4. **Block now stayed asleep past its end once iOS dropped the nap's end activity.**
   - **What happened:** if iOS lost every monitored activity mid-session (Screen Time access
     turned off and on), the next sync shielded the nap's list again (`heldLists` includes a
     running nap), and night windows and limits were re-registered, but nothing
     re-registered `locturne-nap`. With the app closed no `intervalDidEnd` came, so the
     apps slept through the night until Locturne was next opened.
   - **Fix:** `syncLock` now calls a new `rearmNap` (`lib/screen-time.ts`). When a nap is
     running and iOS isn't monitoring `locturne-nap`, it registers a one-off window to the
     nap's end with the same unshield action. The window runs from now, or from 15 minutes
     before the end when less is left (iOS's floor; the start is already past, so iOS
     begins it at once). It is guarded like `startNap` against an end or stand-down mid-registration.
   - **Tests:** `monitoring-recovery.test.ts` (dropped nap re-registered on sync; under 15
     minutes left gets a 15-minute window ending on time).

**Native screen time**
5. **Home's "today" screen-time pill showed yesterday's total after a night in the background.**
   - **What happened:** the pill's `revision` was bumped only by `useFocusEffect`. Home
     left suspended overnight never lost focus, so the report's `DeviceActivityFilter`
     (fixed when `refresh()` ran) still covered yesterday, and "4h 10m today" was
     yesterday's usage. In one day, too, it froze at the time Home was opened. The 7-day
     chart and Usage screen already refreshed on `AppState` 'active'.
   - **Fix:** a new `useVisit(day)` hook (`features/home/use-visit.ts`) bumps the revision on
     focus, on `AppState` 'active', and when `dateKey(now)` changes, so a Home left open
     across midnight rolls over too.
   - **Tests:** `features/home/use-visit.test.ts` (foreground return and day rollover move the
     revision).

**Wake-up**
6. **Each wake-up notification tap stacked another scan screen for scan users.**
   - **What happened:** the tap handler called `router.navigate('/wake')`, which only reuses
     the route when `wake` is the one in front. For a scan routine `/wake` redirects
     (`router.replace`) to `/scan?mode=morning`, so a tap while the scan screen was open
     pushed a new `wake` that turned into a second scan screen. Each tap added one more, and
     Done after a scan revealed an older copy underneath. A tap during "Walk N steps
     instead" (`/wake?method=steps`) cleared the method and sent the walker back to the scan.
   - **Fix:** `useAppStart` keeps the current pathname, and the tap does nothing while a new
     `wakeScreenShowing` (`lib/notifications.ts`) says `/wake` or `/scan` is in front.
   - **Tests:** `notifications.test.ts` (`wakeScreenShowing`).

**Home, sleep and nap (2)**
7. **A nap across a time zone change kept its apps asleep past the end until Locturne opened.**
   - **What happened:** `startNap` and `rearmNap` handed iOS the window as floating clock
     times, which iOS reads in whatever zone the phone is in. After a flight east the end
     came an hour early, the extension ignored it (the stored `end` hadn't passed), and
     nothing else woke the apps. Flying west, the end came hours late.
   - **Fix:** both nap windows now pin their clock times to the zone they were worked out
     in (`timeZoneIdentifier`, a new `napClockOf` in `lib/screen-time.ts`), so iOS ends the
     nap at the stored absolute time.
   - **Tests:** `screen-time.test.ts` (a nap window is pinned to the zone it started in).
8. **Home showed the bedtime countdown during a Block now.**
   - **What happened:** the tabs redesign dropped Home's `blockNowUntil` handling, so mid-nap
     Home read "Bedtime in 9h. Your apps go to sleep at 11:00 PM" with nothing saying the
     apps were asleep. This undid 10-03's "Home shows a running Block now".
   - **Fix:** by day, a running Block now gets a new `napHero` (`features/home/awake-line.ts`):
     "Your apps are asleep / Block now: apps asleep until 3:12 PM." A status that needs
     attention still leads.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`napHero`).
9. **The "mornings you got up" count stopped at 30 and dropped when a pass was used.**
   - **What happened:** Home counted real proofs in the proofs list, which keeps the last 30.
     A pass or emergency joined the same list and pushed the oldest real morning out (30 to
     29), and the count could never pass 30.
   - **Fix:** a monotonic `locturne.morningsWon` counter in the App Group
     (`getMorningsWon`, `lib/morning-proof.ts`). `recordProof` writes it before trimming the
     list, adding one only for a real wake-up on a new morning key. Without the counter yet,
     the proofs on hand seed it. Home's count reads it; the week dots still use the list.
   - **Tests:** `passes.test.ts` (past 30, a pass never takes one away, no double count).

**Money and onboarding (2)**
10. **An Ask to Buy approval that landed off the paywall sent the subscriber back to pay again.**
   - **What happened:** onboarding read `entitled` once, on mount. `approvedLater` (the only
     `onEntitled` handler in the flow) returned early unless the step was `offer`, `plans` or
     `declined`. So a teen who went Back to `commit` while waiting, or a relaunch where the
     SDK synced after `hello` mounted, got the approval dropped: `use-app-start` armed
     tonight, but the commit hold ran `next` into `offer` and `plans`. Picking Monthly there
     is a plan change in the same subscription group, not a no-op.
   - **Fix:** `approvedLater` now calls `setEntitled(true)` and `setNoMoreExitOffer(true)`
     before the step check, so the commit hold finishes through `finishSetup`.
   - **Tests:** `features/onboarding/purchase-lifetime.test.ts` (approval on `commit`/`hello`
     marks the flow entitled without finishing; on `plans` it still finishes).

**Platform (self-check)**
11. **Turning on a night that was off made Home say "Bedtime ran, but nothing slept" for a day.**
   - **What happened:** `readNightChecks` passed today's routine to `checkNights` for all 7
     past nights. On an off night the extension runs `skipLocturneNight`, which still logs an
     unshielded `intervalDidStart`. Turn that evening on (the edit lands at the next bedtime,
     on or off), and the past off night was judged as one that should have locked: `noShield`.
     Home's hero showed the attention line until the next judged night, and `reportNights`
     sent `night_checked {verdict: 'noShield'}`.
   - **Fix:** `checkNights` takes `change` (`getRoutineChange`). A night that ended by
     `change.since` is judged by `prior.activeNights`, the same rule as `nightRanUnder`; with
     no prior routine it isn't checked.
   - **Tests:** `health.test.ts` (Friday turned on from Saturday 23:00: the skipped Friday
     night isn't judged; a later Friday is).

**Wake-up (2)**
12. **A successful morning scan said "awake until bedtime" with tonight off or nothing scheduled.**
   - **What happened:** the scan screen's success body was a fixed "Your bedtime apps are
     awake until bedtime." A scan on a lapse's last paid morning (which `submitScan` allows),
     or with tonight switched off, promised a bedtime that won't lock, while the wake screen
     and Home said "Nothing is scheduled to sleep" / "Tonight is off". Same class as 10-04
     #163 and #36; the scan success path was never moved over.
   - **Fix:** the wake screen's status helper moved to `awakeStatus` in
     `features/wake/awake-status.ts` (nightAt, stood-down or lapse past cover,
     `nightLockArmed`, always list, wrapped in `dayStatus`). Both the wake and scan success

   - **Tests:** `features/scan/scan-screen.test.ts` (runs the real helper: tonight on names
     the time; tonight off and a past lapse don't).

**Home, sleep and nap (3)**
13. **The in-app trial-end notice (B4) was gone from Home.**
   - **What happened:** the tab redesign dropped Home's `TrialNote` and its Manage
     subscription button, so `trialNotice` was imported nowhere. Someone with notifications
     off (or a lost reminder) saw nothing in the trial's last two days before the $59.99
     charge, and #3 above assumed Home's notice still existed.
   - **Fix:** by day, while `trialNotice(getTrialEnd(), now)` returns a notice and nothing
     needs fixing (unprotected, attention, paused), Home's hero is its title and detail (a new
     `trialHero` in `features/home/awake-line.ts`) and the action is "Manage subscription"
     (Apple's sheet, its web page as fallback, as the You tab does).
   - **Tests:** new `features/home/home-screen.test.ts` renders Home with its stores mocked:
     the day before and the day of the charge lead with the notice; outside the window, with
     attention, or on a morning, Home is unchanged.
14. **A night that's switched off said "Nothing sleeps tonight" while a Block now or the always list held apps.**
   - **What happened:** on an off night `lock.phase` is `'off'`, and `heldPhase` leaves it so.
     `napHero` only ran for `'day'`, and the `'off'` hero was a fixed "Nothing sleeps tonight.
     I'm sleeping anyway." A Block now started at 22:30 on an off Friday, or any apps on the
     always-asleep list, were shielded under that line (round 7 #46's rule was lost).
   - **Fix:** a running Block now leads on `'off'` as by day. A new `offHero` says "Bedtime
     apps awake tonight. Always-asleep apps still sleep." when the always list has apps and
     isn't stood down, and keeps the old line otherwise.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`offHero`); `home-screen.test.ts` (Block now leads on an
     off night, the always list is named, an ended Block now doesn't lead).

**Tabs**
15. **Adding bedtime apps on the Apps tab never armed a night left unarmed by an empty bedtime list.**
   - **What happened:** with no bedtime-list apps, `armIfPaid` skips arming
     (`shownSelection('night').size === 0`). That state follows a lapse and renewal, or
     turning every night off and on again. The Apps tab's `pickedList` copied newly added
     apps live and called `reapplyStandingBlocks`, but neither arms a night, and `syncLock`
     only re-arms an armed one. The tab said "Apps sleep 11:00 PM" while iOS had no night
     windows, so nothing slept and the next morning wasn't locked either, until Locturne
     was reopened.
   - **Fix:** `pickedList` calls `armIfPaid()` after a bedtime-list edit when
     `getArmedNight()` is null, as the Routine tab's save does (still subscription-gated).
   - **Tests:** `bypass-findings.test.ts` F5 (emptied list, lapse and renewal, apps added at
     21:30 with no reopen: asleep at 22:30).

**Money and onboarding (3)**
16. **The onboarding sample notification always said "Tonight, at <bedtime>".**
   - **What happened:** `sendFirstNote` hard-coded "Tonight". A night-shift user (bed 8:00 AM)
     finishing in the afternoon heard "Tonight, at 8:00 AM" under "Armed. Tomorrow at
     8:00 AM.", and an all-nights-off rerun (`openRoutine`) got a sample for a night that
     never comes. (The re-reported missing Home trial notice was already #13.)
   - **Fix:** `scheduleCopy` has a new `note` ("Tomorrow at 8:00 AM", the day `armed` names),
     null with every night off or a night already started. `ask` sends the sample only when

   - **Tests:** `schedule-copy.test.ts` (night shift says tomorrow, evening says today,
     nights off and a started night send none).

**Home, sleep and nap (4)**
17. **A day whose night was off counted down to a later bedtime as if it were tonight.**
   - **What happened:** with Friday and Saturday nights off, Home on Friday morning read
     "Bedtime in 61h / Your apps go to sleep at 11:00 PM". `sleepsAt` is the next night that's
     on (Sunday), and `dayHero` names no day, so it read as tonight while the wake and scan
     screens said tonight is off (the 10-04 #33 class, back on Home after the tab redesign).
   - **Fix:** Home checks tonight's own night (`nightAt(nextBedtime(now))`). When it's off,
     `dayHero` gets the next night's weekday (`offTonight`) and says "Tonight is off / Your
     next bedtime is 11:00 PM on Sunday." `awakeLine` gets no bedtime then, as `awakeStatus` does.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`dayHero` with tonight off); `home-screen.test.ts`
     (Friday with the weekend off names Sunday, no "Bedtime in"; a night that's on still counts down).
18. **After an emergency unlock, a Block now started on the paused night was hidden behind "Awake tonight".**
   - **What happened:** the bedtime list is parked after an emergency, but `blocker()` still
     allows a Block now on picked apps. Home checked the pause before `napping`, so a 23:45
     Block now until 00:45 showed "Awake tonight / your apps are awake tonight" while the
     picked apps slept (the #14 class, on the paused branch).
   - **Fix:** the paused hero only shows with no Block now running. Otherwise `napHero` leads,
     as by day and on a night off (`heldPhase` already reads a paused night as day).
   - **Tests:** `home-screen.test.ts` (a Block now on a paused night leads; without one,
     "Awake tonight" stays).

**Platform (analytics)**
19. **Analytics never started on any install since the age question was cut.**
   - **What happened:** `startAnalytics` (`0fdcb2d`) only built PostHog once
     `locturne.analytics.eligible` was `true`, and only an `age_bracket` answer wrote that.
     The age step was removed about an hour later (`0b735ad`), so every install since
     2026-10-05 kept its events in the 50-item local queue and dropped them: no funnels, no
     retention, no error reports, and no `$posthogUserId` on RevenueCat.
   - **Fix:** with no age asked, a missing answer is allowed. `startAnalytics` initializes
     unless the stored value is `false`, so an older install that said under 13 stays stopped.
   - **Tests:** `analytics-start.test.ts` (a fresh install initializes and sends its funnel;
     a stored `false` stays stopped across launches; any other stored value starts).

**Wake-up (3)**
20. **Downstairs kept saying Motion & Fitness was off after it was turned on from its own Open Settings.**
   - **What happened:** `watchDownstairs` read the permission once, at creation, and only
     `start()` read it again, but the denied view has no Start button. A user who turned
     Motion & Fitness on from MotionOff's Open Settings came back to "I can't feel the
     stairs." until the wake screen was reopened. That left 'Walk N steps instead', the scan,
     a pass or the emergency unlock as the only ways forward. Same class as the step-watch
     item, in a different file.
   - **Fix:** the watch now listens for `AppState` 'active' for its whole life (removed in
     `dispose`). On return it reads `getPermissionsAsync` again and reports `ready` or
     `denied` when the answer changed. It skips this while Start is asking, before the first
     check settles, and for `unavailable`.
   - **Tests:** `downstairs-watch.test.ts` (denied, then permission granted and 'active',
     gives ready and Start runs; turned off while away gives denied; no barometer stays
     unavailable; a disposed watch stops checking).

**Tabs**
21. **Setting an earlier bedtime by spinning the hour, then the minutes, locked in the in-between time for tonight.**
   - **What happened:** the compact time picker calls `onDateChange` each time a wheel
     settles, and TimeRow saved every one. Going 11:00 PM to 9:45 PM at 9:20 PM saved 9:00 PM
     first; its first night was already running, so it armed and shielded at once. The 9:45
     save then ran with `early` true, promoted 9:00 PM to the routine in force and pushed
     9:45 PM to tomorrow. Apps slept 25 minutes early with nothing short of the emergency
     unlock to undo it, under a banner naming 9:45 PM.
   - **Fix:** `TimeRow` (`controls.ios.tsx`) holds the picked time in a `settler`
     (`lib/settle.ts`) and saves it 800 ms after the last wheel change, or at once when the
     row unmounts. Only the final time reaches `commit`, and the refusal check runs on it.
   - **Tests:** `settle.test.ts` (hour then minutes saves only 9:45 PM, once; leaving the
     row saves the held time once).

**Platform (self-check 2)**
22. **A night the full heartbeat log had cut off partway through read as 'late' by hours, and analytics sent it.**
   - **What happened:** the Swift log keeps 100 entries (about 22 a day at 23:00 to 07:00,
     more with limits and naps). Away from Oct 4 to Oct 9, the oldest kept entry was about
     Oct 5 02:38, so the night into Oct 5 kept only its later windows. `checkNights` only
     checked `coverageStart` when no start survived, so it took the 02:38 window as the first
     and said `late`, `lateBy` 262, though every window ran on time. `reportNights` then sent
     `night_checked late` for the users who stop opening the app.
   - **Fix:** a night starting before `coverageStart` (less `EARLY_SLACK`) is `unknown`,
     never late or noShield, unless the log still holds a protected start within
     `ON_TIME_GRACE` of bedtime, which keeps `onTime`.
   - **Tests:** `health.test.ts` (a log cut at 02:38 gives unknown, an unshielded survivor is
     not noShield, a cut that kept the bedtime start is still on time).
