## Fixed

**Home, sleep and nap**
1. **Home promised "Your apps go to sleep at 11:00 PM" when nothing would sleep.**
   - **What happened:** the tab redesign (`0206478`) dropped Home's health note, See plans
     and `awakeLine`. A user with no subscription (lapsed and stood down, never bought), an
     Ask to Buy wait, or a night iOS refused to arm read "Bedtime in 8h. Your apps go to
     sleep at 11:00 PM". At 23:30 the unheld night read as day, so it counted down to
     tomorrow's bedtime instead.
   - **Fix:** by day (including a night `heldPhase` reads as day), Home's hero comes from a
     new pure `dayHero` (`features/home/awake-line.ts`). When `rollUpHealth` needs attention,
     its title and detail lead, and the button becomes "See plans" when subscribing is the
     fix. Otherwise, with no night lock scheduled, the body is `awakeLine` ("Nothing is
     scheduled to sleep") and no bedtime is named.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`dayHero`).
2. **The nap ruler offered 5 and 10 minutes, which iOS always refuses.**
   - **What happened:** the Sleep sheet's ruler (`f05fac1`) started at 5 minutes.
     DeviceActivity refuses windows under 15 minutes (`intervalTooShort`), so those naps
     always failed with "iOS wouldn't start the nap. Try again in a moment."
   - **Fix:** `NAP_MIN` now comes from `NAP_SHORTEST` (15) in `lib/screen-time.ts`, and
     `startNap` refuses anything shorter before asking iOS.
   - **Tests:** `screen-time.test.ts` (a nap under 15 minutes is refused).

**Money and onboarding**
3. **A purchase that finished after the paywall was closed never kept "Remind me".**
   - **What happened:** the paywall's X stays live while RevenueCat posts the receipt. Closing
     it then saved the setup (`exit()`), so when the purchase resolved `purchased` or
     `pending`, the late branches in `finishSetup` and `buy` skipped `saveTrialReminder`
     behind `!hasRoutine()`. `syncTrialEnd` only schedules when `TRIAL_REMINDER_KEY` is true,
     so no trial-end reminder and no Home notice came before the $59.99 charge. A See plans
     rerun always has a routine, so every late purchase there was hit.
   - **Fix:** the `!hasRoutine()` guard now covers only `saveSetup` (keeping later Routine
     edits). The reminder choice is always saved, in both late branches.
   - **Tests:** `features/onboarding/purchase-lifetime.test.ts` (late success and late Ask to
     Buy both save the reminder).

**Lock core**
4. **Block now stayed asleep past its end once iOS dropped the nap's end activity.**
   - **What happened:** if iOS lost every monitored activity mid-session (Screen Time access
     turned off and on), the next sync shielded the nap's list again (`heldLists` includes a
     running nap), and night windows and limits were re-registered, but nothing
     re-registered `locturne-nap`. With the app closed no `intervalDidEnd` came, so the
     apps slept through the night until Locturne was next opened.
   - **Fix:** `syncLock` now calls a new `rearmNap` (`lib/screen-time.ts`). When a nap is
     running and iOS isn't monitoring `locturne-nap`, it registers a one-off window to the
     nap's end with the same unshield action. The window runs from now, or from 15 minutes
     before the end when less is left (iOS's floor; the start is already past, so iOS
     begins it at once). It is guarded like `startNap` against an end or stand-down mid-registration.
   - **Tests:** `monitoring-recovery.test.ts` (dropped nap re-registered on sync; under 15
     minutes left gets a 15-minute window ending on time).

**Native screen time**
5. **Home's "today" screen-time pill showed yesterday's total after a night in the background.**
   - **What happened:** the pill's `revision` was bumped only by `useFocusEffect`. Home
     left suspended overnight never lost focus, so the report's `DeviceActivityFilter`
     (fixed when `refresh()` ran) still covered yesterday, and "4h 10m today" was
     yesterday's usage. In one day, too, it froze at the time Home was opened. The 7-day
     chart and Usage screen already refreshed on `AppState` 'active'.
   - **Fix:** a new `useVisit(day)` hook (`features/home/use-visit.ts`) bumps the revision on
     focus, on `AppState` 'active', and when `dateKey(now)` changes, so a Home left open
     across midnight rolls over too.
   - **Tests:** `features/home/use-visit.test.ts` (foreground return and day rollover move the
     revision).

**Wake-up**
6. **Each wake-up notification tap stacked another scan screen for scan users.**
   - **What happened:** the tap handler called `router.navigate('/wake')`, which only reuses
     the route when `wake` is the one in front. For a scan routine `/wake` redirects
     (`router.replace`) to `/scan?mode=morning`, so a tap while the scan screen was open
     pushed a new `wake` that turned into a second scan screen. Each tap added one more, and
     Done after a scan revealed an older copy underneath. A tap during "Walk N steps
     instead" (`/wake?method=steps`) cleared the method and sent the walker back to the scan.
   - **Fix:** `useAppStart` keeps the current pathname, and the tap does nothing while a new
     `wakeScreenShowing` (`lib/notifications.ts`) says `/wake` or `/scan` is in front.
   - **Tests:** `notifications.test.ts` (`wakeScreenShowing`).
