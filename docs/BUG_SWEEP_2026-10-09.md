## Fixed

**Home, sleep and nap**
1. **Home promised "Your apps go to sleep at 11:00 PM" when nothing would sleep.**
   - **What happened:** the tab redesign (`0206478`) dropped Home's health note, See plans
     and `awakeLine`. A user with no subscription (lapsed and stood down, never bought), an
     Ask to Buy wait, or a night iOS refused to arm read "Bedtime in 8h. Your apps go to
     sleep at 11:00 PM". At 23:30 the unheld night read as day, so it counted down to
     tomorrow's bedtime instead.
   - **Fix:** by day (including a night `heldPhase` reads as day), Home's hero comes from a
     new pure `dayHero` (`features/home/awake-line.ts`). When `rollUpHealth` needs attention,
     its title and detail lead, and the button becomes "See plans" when subscribing is the
     fix. Otherwise, with no night lock scheduled, the body is `awakeLine` ("Nothing is
     scheduled to sleep") and no bedtime is named.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`dayHero`).
2. **The nap ruler offered 5 and 10 minutes, which iOS always refuses.**
   - **What happened:** the Sleep sheet's ruler (`f05fac1`) started at 5 minutes.
     DeviceActivity refuses windows under 15 minutes (`intervalTooShort`), so those naps
     always failed with "iOS wouldn't start the nap. Try again in a moment."
   - **Fix:** `NAP_MIN` now comes from `NAP_SHORTEST` (15) in `lib/screen-time.ts`, and
     `startNap` refuses anything shorter before asking iOS.
   - **Tests:** `screen-time.test.ts` (a nap under 15 minutes is refused).
