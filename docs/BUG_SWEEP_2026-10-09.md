# Bug sweep, 2026-10-09 (overnight)

An automated loop on branch `bug-sweep/2026-10-09`, starting at `47e36a9`. Each sweep
covers the whole app with 8 area finders, using a different lens each time. Every finding
gets two adversarial verifiers before a fixer touches it. The loop stops after **3 sweeps
in a row with no confirmed bugs**, or after 8 sweeps at most.

Earlier sweeps: [2026-10-03](BUG_SWEEP_2026-10-03.md), [2026-10-04](BUG_SWEEP_2026-10-04.md).

## Fixed

**Home, sleep and nap**
1. **Home promised "Your apps go to sleep at 11:00 PM" when nothing would sleep.**
   - **What happened:** the tab redesign (`0206478`) dropped Home's health note, See plans
     and `awakeLine`. A user with no subscription (lapsed and stood down, never bought), an
     Ask to Buy wait, or a night iOS refused to arm read "Bedtime in 8h. Your apps go to
     sleep at 11:00 PM". At 23:30 the unheld night read as day, so it counted down to
     tomorrow's bedtime instead.
   - **Fix:** by day (including a night `heldPhase` reads as day), Home's hero comes from a
     new pure `dayHero` (`features/home/awake-line.ts`). When `rollUpHealth` needs attention,
     its title and detail lead, and the button becomes "See plans" when subscribing is the
     fix. Otherwise, with no night lock scheduled, the body is `awakeLine` ("Nothing is
     scheduled to sleep") and no bedtime is named.
   - **Tests:** `awake-line.test.ts` (`dayHero`).
2. **The nap ruler offered 5 and 10 minutes, which iOS always refuses.**
   - **What happened:** the Sleep sheet's ruler (`f05fac1`) started at 5 minutes.
     DeviceActivity refuses windows under 15 minutes (`intervalTooShort`), so those naps
     always failed with "iOS wouldn't start the nap. Try again in a moment."
   - **Fix:** `NAP_MIN` now comes from `NAP_SHORTEST` (15) in `lib/screen-time.ts`, and
     `startNap` refuses anything shorter before asking iOS.
   - **Tests:** `screen-time.test.ts` (a nap under 15 minutes is refused).

**Money and onboarding**
3. **A purchase that finished after the paywall was closed never kept "Remind me".**
   - **What happened:** the paywall's X stays live while RevenueCat posts the receipt. Closing
     it then saved the setup (`exit()`), so when the purchase resolved `purchased` or
     `pending`, the late branches in `finishSetup` and `buy` skipped `saveTrialReminder`
     behind `!hasRoutine()`. `syncTrialEnd` only schedules when `TRIAL_REMINDER_KEY` is true,
     so no trial-end reminder and no Home notice came before the $59.99 charge. A See plans
     rerun always has a routine, so every late purchase there was hit.
   - **Fix:** the `!hasRoutine()` guard now covers only `saveSetup` (keeping later Routine
     edits). The reminder choice is always saved, in both late branches.
   - **Tests:** `features/onboarding/purchase-lifetime.test.ts` (late success and late Ask to
     Buy both save the reminder).

**Lock core**
4. **Block now stayed asleep past its end once iOS dropped the nap's end activity.**
   - **What happened:** if iOS lost every monitored activity mid-session (Screen Time access
     turned off and on), the next sync shielded the nap's list again (`heldLists` includes a
     running nap), and night windows and limits were re-registered, but nothing
     re-registered `locturne-nap`. With the app closed no `intervalDidEnd` came, so the
     apps slept through the night until Locturne was next opened.
   - **Fix:** `syncLock` now calls a new `rearmNap` (`lib/screen-time.ts`). When a nap is
     running and iOS isn't monitoring `locturne-nap`, it registers a one-off window to the
     nap's end with the same unshield action. The window runs from now, or from 15 minutes
     before the end when less is left (iOS's floor; the start is already past, so iOS
     begins it at once). It is guarded like `startNap` against an end or stand-down mid-registration.
   - **Tests:** `monitoring-recovery.test.ts` (dropped nap re-registered on sync; under 15
     minutes left gets a 15-minute window ending on time).

**Native screen time**
5. **Home's "today" screen-time pill showed yesterday's total after a night in the background.**
   - **What happened:** the pill's `revision` was bumped only by `useFocusEffect`. Home
     left suspended overnight never lost focus, so the report's `DeviceActivityFilter`
     (fixed when `refresh()` ran) still covered yesterday, and "4h 10m today" was
     yesterday's usage. In one day, too, it froze at the time Home was opened. The 7-day
     chart and Usage screen already refreshed on `AppState` 'active'.
   - **Fix:** a new `useVisit(day)` hook (`features/home/use-visit.ts`) bumps the revision on
     focus, on `AppState` 'active', and when `dateKey(now)` changes, so a Home left open
     across midnight rolls over too.
   - **Tests:** `features/home/use-visit.test.ts` (foreground return and day rollover move the
     revision).

**Wake-up**
6. **Each wake-up notification tap stacked another scan screen for scan users.**
   - **What happened:** the tap handler called `router.navigate('/wake')`, which only reuses
     the route when `wake` is the one in front. For a scan routine `/wake` redirects
     (`router.replace`) to `/scan?mode=morning`, so a tap while the scan screen was open
     pushed a new `wake` that turned into a second scan screen. Each tap added one more, and
     Done after a scan revealed an older copy underneath. A tap during "Walk N steps
     instead" (`/wake?method=steps`) cleared the method and sent the walker back to the scan.
   - **Fix:** `useAppStart` keeps the current pathname, and the tap does nothing while a new
     `wakeScreenShowing` (`lib/notifications.ts`) says `/wake` or `/scan` is in front.
   - **Tests:** `notifications.test.ts` (`wakeScreenShowing`).

**Home, sleep and nap (2)**
7. **A nap across a time zone change kept its apps asleep past the end until Locturne opened.**

     with bedtime 23:00, a VoiceOver user heading for 21:30 who overshot one step to 21:15
     saved 21:15, which started tonight and shielded at once; the 21:30 correction then landed
     inside that night and waited for tomorrow. The drag path only sends on release, and
     `TimeRow` got the settler in #28, but the dial's step path had neither.
   - **Fix:** `nudge` now holds the step (`heldStep`) and sends 3 s after the last swipe
     (`stepSettle`, matching `SETTLE_MS`). A held step is sent at once when focus leaves the
     handle (`accessibilityElementDidLoseFocus`), the dial leaves the window, or the app
     resigns active; props don't overwrite a held step, and a drag's release sends it too.
   - **Tests:** none automated: the dial is UIKit, which `native-tests` can't build on Linux
     (Swift parse-checked; `npm test`, `tsc`, lint and `test:native` pass). Check on device
     with VoiceOver: swipe the bedtime handle a few steps and confirm one save.

**Tabs (4)**
31. **A waiting removal settling while Apple's picker was open made closing it unchanged empty the list.**
   - **What happened:** with a bedtime-list removal waiting for 11:00 PM, a user opened "Add
     or remove apps" at 10:59 PM. Bedtime started and the extension (or the app's
     `settleListChanges`) swapped the draft live and removed it. Cancel, or Done with no
     change, writes nothing, so `finishListEdit` saw a live list, no waiting change and no
     draft, and recorded `empty: true` for the next bedtime: every bedtime app was wiped and
     nothing slept from then on. Same for the always list, a daily limit, and a paused night
     whose pause ended with the picker open.
   - **Fix:** `beginListEdit` remembers whether the edit opened on a waiting change
     (`editBegunOnWaiting`). If that change has settled by `finishListEdit` and no draft was
     written since, the edit is a no-op ('now'): the list already is what the picker showed.
     An edit that empties the list with nothing waiting still records `empty`.
   - **Tests:** `list-edit-races.test.ts` (removal waiting, picker opened, settled at
     bedtime, closed unchanged: returns 'now', nothing pending, picks kept).

**Platform (2)**
32. **Bedtime-list edits on the Apps tab never re-planned tonight's notifications.**
   - **What happened:** `pickedList` (picker close and swipe-to-remove) never called
     `rescheduleNotifications`, and nothing else re-plans on a list edit. Emptying the list
     at 20:00 still left "Bedtime in 15 minutes. Then your apps sleep." and the 07:00
     "apps stay asleep" note, which opened the wake screen with nothing asleep. Refilling an
     emptied list on an armed night got no bedtime warning and no morning note.
   - **Fix:** `pickedList` re-plans (`rescheduleNotifications().catch(() => {})`) after a
     bedtime-list edit, as the Routine and You tabs do. `planNotifications` already reads the
     list as it will stand at bedtime.
   - **Tests:** `apps-list.test.ts` (a bedtime-list edit re-plans; a limit edit doesn't).
33. **A wake-up notification tapped with the Exits sheet open stacked a second wake or scan screen.**
   - **What happened:** Exits is pushed as a sheet over `/wake` or `/scan`. Tapping the morning
     or shield-tap note there read the pathname `/exits`, so `router.navigate('/wake')` ran;
     NAVIGATE only reuses the top route, so it pushed a new wake (redirecting to a second
     scan). Done after scanning went back to the stale sheet and the first scan screen.
   - **Fix:** `wakeScreenShowing` treats `/exits` as part of the wake flow, so the tap leaves
     the stack alone. (Exits opened from You or a nap also stays put; closing it shows where
     the person was.)
   - **Tests:** `notifications.test.ts` (`/exits` now counts).

34. **An emergency unlock in a lapse's last paid night promised the apps sleep again at the next bedtime.**
   - **What happened:** Lapse found Mon 23:30 (Mon night and Tue morning finish), then an
     emergency unlock: Exits, Home and the Apps note said "They go back to sleep at 11:00 PM",
     but Tuesday night is past the last paid morning, so the extension skips it and nothing sleeps.
   - **Fix:** `pauseWording` returns `resumes: null` and `ended: true` when the next night on
     is `pastLastPaid`; `describe()`, `pausedHero` and `pauseNote` then say there's no
     subscription, so nothing sleeps after that.
   - **Tests:** `past-last-paid.test.ts` (lapse + unlock names no bedtime, Tuesday really stays
     awake; subscribed still names 23:00), `pending-note.test.ts` (lapse line).

**Native (2)**
35. **A touch on a night dial handle moved a routine set off the quarter hour, and saved it.**
   - **What happened:** `move(to:)` snapped the night's length, not the dragged handle's time.
     With 10:50 PM to 7:00 AM (the Routine pickers step by a minute), any touch movement on
     the sun handle gave span 490 → 495, so morning start saved as 7:05 AM; the moon gave an
     earlier 10:45 PM bedtime that applied at once. Drags only reached :05/:20/:35/:50 and
     never the original time.
   - **Fix:** a new `endSnapped` puts the dragged handle's own time on a quarter hour (then
     keeps the span between `minWindow` and a day less a step), and under half a step of
     movement leaves both times as they were, like the `.night` case, so nothing is sent.
   - **Tests:** none automated: the dial is UIKit, which `native-tests` can't build on Linux.
     `endSnapped` was compiled and checked in a scratch Swift file (10:50 PM to 7:00 AM drags
     land on :00/:15/:30/:45; clamp edges with an off-grid fixed end); `npm test`, `tsc`, lint
     and `test:native(:tz)` pass. Check on device: set 10:50 PM, touch and release a handle,
     confirm no save.

**Wake-up (4)**
36. **Steps stayed on "Motion & Fitness is off" after the user turned it on from its own Open Settings.**
   - **What happened:** `watchSteps` asked for the permission once at open; when denied, `count`
     stayed null and the app-state handler returned straight away, so coming back from
     Settings never read the permission again. The steps screen (and onboarding's practice
     walk) said "I can't hear you walking." until reopened, leaving only a pass or the
     emergency unlock. The step-watch version of #20 (downstairs).
   - **Fix:** while denied, a return to `active` calls `getPermissionsAsync` (added to
     `StepSensor`); if granted it reports `starting` and runs the same start as on open
     (history, `startCount`, `counting`, subscribe, poll). An in-flight flag means two quick
     returns start one watcher; a stop meanwhile starts nothing.
   - **Tests:** `step-watch.test.ts` (still off stays denied; turned on → `denied, starting,
     counting` with one watcher, one poll and no second prompt; stopped mid re-read starts nothing).
