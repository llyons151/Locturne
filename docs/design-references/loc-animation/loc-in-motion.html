What the research said, and where it shows up

Act with the outline

A silhouette has no face, so ears, head tilt and posture carry everything. Each state here changes at least three of them at once, so it reads even at 32 pt.

Idle on its own layer

Rive mascots blink and glance on a random timer every 2 to 5 seconds, separate from the app’s commands. Same here: the app only sets the state.

Ears lag the head

Follow-through: the ears are softer springs than the head, so they overshoot and settle after every move. That wobble is most of the life.

Eyes as cut-outs

The eye slits are holes, so the moon shows through. The pupils follow your pointer, like the eye-tracking Rive characters do with touch.

Restraint

No particles, no glow, no bounce for its own sake. Breathing is 1 to 2 percent. Reduced-motion turns the idle layer off.

Same API as the app will use

One rig, a state and a few triggers. Swapping this for a Rive file later keeps every screen’s code the same.
