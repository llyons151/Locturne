## Why ours looks off (diagnosis)

1. Pure black card on a dark navy sky reads as a hole cut in the page, not a surface. Every
   reference uses a dark grey (~#1C1C1E) card on black, or separate grey cards.
2. Two selection signals: the picked row's icon tile turns white *and* its radio fills. The
   white tile is the brightest thing on the screen and pulls the eye away from the radio.
3. The "Recommended" pill sits inline with the title and crowds it; references put tags
   below the subtitle or not at all.
4. The heading is a centred, grey body-size line floating over a left-aligned list.
